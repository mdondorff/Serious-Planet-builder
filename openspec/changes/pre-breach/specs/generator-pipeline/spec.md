## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: GEN-174 The closed basins of the start surface are breached at round 0, to the fixed point
The breach is OFF by default (architect, 2026-11-12, pending a redesign): the default plan, the bake profile and the examples SHALL NOT set `pre_breach`; the measured effect of the profile on the production path was worse than none (straight runs over 10 cells 3.30x to 8.0x the DEM on R1h, 6.19x to 9.4x on R3a, 1.83x to 3.8x on R3b; the cap of 8 passes reached on all three; R1h keeps 7.07 % of land in depressions deeper than 100 m), and the hypotheses to test in the redesign are (a) the cost per entered cell makes a diagonal step as cheap as an axial one, (b) path cells lowered exactly to the floor make a flat channel that the fill treats as a flat and the carve incises along, (c) each pass opens nested basins. The mechanism and its tests stay in the tree. When `CarveParams::pre_breach` is set, the carve, the round solver and the memoised solve SHALL, before the first round and before the taper is read, drain each closed basin of the start surface by constrained least-cost breaching, in passes that repeat until one drains no basin (the fixed point), at most `passes` (the profile's cap is 8; reaching it SHALL be reported in the build's MARK line and is an error in a tier A fixture). A basin is a flat of the canonical filled surface (GEN-173) lifted by at least `min_lift_m` (0.5 m, the pit limit of H1); its floor is its lowest cell by (height, id). From the floor the path SHALL run over the 8 neighbours by least cost, entering a cell costing `max(0, z - floor)`, never entering a cell more than `max_depth_m` (the profile's breach limit, 500 m) above the floor, with at most `halo - 1 - diameter` cells (`halo` the profile's halo, 128 cells; `diameter` the basin's Chebyshev diameter in cells), ending at the first cell outside the basin that drains for certain (a cell standing below the floor whose filled level on the pass's flood is also below it, or an outlet at or below the floor; an outlet is never entered otherwise); the path is the lexicographically least (cost, cells) label pruned at the length bound; ties break by id. Every path cell above the floor SHALL be lowered to the floor; the exit is not touched; nothing is raised. A basin that touches or lies beside the border of the grid, has no path length left, or has no path within the depth and the length SHALL stay filled (a lake) and untouched. Basins are processed in order of (floor height, floor id), each on the surface the earlier ones left. The breach of the rounds SHALL lower a cell, however many donors breach it in one round, by at most its limit (`breach_max_m`, or the height-scaled limit) at its height at the start of the round: the sum of the round's breach lowering at a cell is bounded, not each event (a declared change of results where two events met; R1 had a cell lowered by 794 m). The result SHALL be a function of the cells only: the same bits in the whole-region carve, the round solver on any thread count and the memoised solve on any tile size; a closed bowl with no lower cell (GEN-162) SHALL be unchanged. Planar grids only. With the flag off every result and key SHALL be the bits of the build before this requirement, except where the cap of the round's breach (above) binds; pass 2, K7 and a face-scale carve SHALL refuse the flag (the pass is region-wide, its arrays are the region's size and the tile key has no byte for it); the layer key SHALL hold the parameters (byte 0xC7) only when set.

#### Scenario: A noise basin and a deep basin
- **WHEN** a pit of 8 m on a slope and a pit deeper than the breach limit are breached
- **THEN** the first is drained by the two rim cells west of its floor and no closed basin is left, and the second is not touched
