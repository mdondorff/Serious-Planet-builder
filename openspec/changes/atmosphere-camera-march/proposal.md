## Why
SPIKE-20 (`spikes/owner-captures-2026-10-20/results/results.md`): the owner sees rings and bands in the atmosphere above about 20 km after #404.
- The aerial-perspective volume (32 slices, 32 rows, trilinear) is too coarse for rays that end 300 to 1,100 km away: between its texels it is 18 to 23 % off at 60 to 99.5 km and 10 % off at 14 km; the bound of ATMO-007 is 5 %, checked only at texel centres.
- The orbit march is within 0.6 % at the same places, so the bands appear only inside the atmosphere.

## What changes
- From 6 km camera altitude inside the atmosphere a pixel with geometry is hazed by a mix of the volume and a march from the camera to the geometry (the smoothstep of the altitude from 6 km to 10 km, so the haze does not step when the camera crosses: opus-planner's order after the first review), above 10 km by the march alone (`FrameGeometry::camera_ray_cosines`, shader `atm_camera_ray`), not by the volume: 8 steps by day, 12 in twilight, dense towards the geometry (`StepLayout::EndDense`), per-channel transmittance as the orbit march has it, at half the resolution of the frame with a depth-aware upsample. At 6 km and below, and for sky pixels, nothing changes. The altitudes (not 20 km) follow from the measurement: the volume is 7.5 to 10 % off from 10 km to 18 km.
- The step counts follow from the accuracy and the cost: the camera march must be within 5 % + 1e-4 of the converged march for every sun and side (the review of the change: at one sun the centre column only looks towards the sun). The sweep over suns 0.7 to -0.08, towards and away, heights 8 to 99.5 km: 6 steps leave 4.8 of the bound, 8 steps 0.9 by day (sun at least 0.1), 12 steps 0.8 in twilight; 8 and 12 are used (`CAMERA_MARCH_STEPS_DAY`, `CAMERA_MARCH_STEPS_TWILIGHT`). Cost per pixel (indicative, `adhoc_cost_of_the_atmosphere_passes_at_1440p`, RTX, half of the pixels geometry): 40 steps 6.5 ms, 8 steps about 1.8 ms, 12 steps about 2.6 ms over the volume lookup; hence the half-resolution haze.
- The cost, measured (window 21, GPU timestamps, 1440p, aerial 20 km): the per-pixel march took the atmosphere to 1.12 to 1.15 ms by day and 1.65 ms in twilight (the composite pass 1.0 and 1.5 ms against 0.14); at half the resolution (`atmosphere-haze`: a compute pass before the composite, inside its timed span, two `Rgba16Float` targets of half the size, a depth-aware upsample that marches a pixel itself at a depth edge) it is 0.60 to 0.63 ms by day and 0.76 ms in twilight, against 0.25 ms with the volume (ground 1 m 0.26 against 0.21 ms, orbit unchanged): within the 1 ms of ADR 0042. The frame-target share grows by 14.7 MB at 1440p (the graph's `haze-lowres`).
- Specs: ATMO-001 (the camera march is matched by the GPU), ATMO-002 (the composite's rule), ATMO-007 (the bounds of the camera march and of the volume between its texels), ATMO-021 (the rule, the across-the-top bound from 10 % to 5 %, measured 3.5 %).
- Tests: tier A (`planet-frame`): the haze continuous across the blend band (the error against the converged march changes by at most 0.48 % per 100 m, bound 1 %); the camera march and the volume between its texels against the converged march; across the top; tier B (`oracle_atmosphere`): the composite above 8 km against the reference; the shader's constants against the reference's; the upsample reference `haze_upsample`.

Expected golden changes:

Bound tests:

The sky goldens are not blessed by the tool: they go to the owner's `/bless`. The render graph test expectations carry the haze's 14,745,600 B at 1440p. The pixel-hash tables of `gpu_compact_blocks` (the lit atmosphere frame) are re-recorded for WARP and RTX.

Not covered: terrain past the tangent point (up to 6 % by day, 24 to 39 % in twilight off the converged march), and the volume's own error at a low sun below 8 km (17 to 18 % at 2 km), which the march does not replace; 
Also not covered: the haze radiance is stored in `Rgba16Float` like the volume and the sky-view table (in deep twilight the values near 1e-6 carry a few percent of quantisation); a pixel whose texels sit about 1.5 tolerances from its distance switches from the average to its own march (the haze steps by a few percent of its transmittance there).
