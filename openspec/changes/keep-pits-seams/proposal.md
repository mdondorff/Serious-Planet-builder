## Why
The keep-pits preview (inbox #66, seed 14, production grid) found the tile-local fill depth of #1192 seam-inconsistent in the mountains: across the seam 2/12 | 2/13 the fill adds a median 152 m (max 255 m) to the height step at pit cells, while the unfilled surfaces agree. A basin wider than the 128-cell halo is closed in one tile's window and drains through the window's border (an outlet) in the next. Pass 1 has no pit at those cells, so no larger-scale input repairs it. ADR 0101 needs a seam-consistent fill of the deep mountain basins (options (b) and (c) leave exactly those open or only move the seam).

## What Changes
Option (a) of the report, split in three changes of one branch each (planner, 2026-11-05):
- **PR 1 (merged, #1197): GEN-121, the kernel.** `generators::bake::spill`: `flood_labelled` (a tile's priority flood from its outlets and its border cells with territory labels), `SpillGraph` (the spill graph over tiles, tile edges, cube edges and corners named by the caller; the planet-wide level of every seed), `final_level`. Tested against a global minimax flood as the oracle. No store, no format, no bake behaviour changes.
- PR 2 (merged, #1199): STRM-077 and the amendments of GEN-120 (the depth is the planet-wide level) and STRM-076 (kind 17 is 16-bit codes at 0.25 m, written by the post-pass; kind 16 is not used). Pass 2 leaves its final heights (f32, exact: the scratch is deleted anyway) as bake scratch (kind 18); `layer_fillpass::run_fill_pass` floods each core, joins the tiles over tile edges, cube edges and corners (`bake::fillgeom`), solves the spill graph and writes kind 17; `bake layer` runs it after pass 2 and deletes the scratch. Pass 2's window flood and pass 1's face fill are removed. `BAKE_KERNEL_VERSION` and `PASS2_KERNEL_VERSION` do not move (the stored changes are the same bits); `CHANNELS_VERSION` 3 re-keys kinds 14 and 15 as well (a store from before is refused, not rebaked; #1192 never went into a bake), and the post-pass kernels have their own `FILL_KERNEL_VERSION` in the fill and scratch keys. The 0.25 m depth code and the exact-base depth are the owner's decision (2026-11-05).
- **PR 3 (this one):** the depth is taken against the exact base plus the stored change at the pass-2 pit cells, clamped at 0 (owner and planner 2026-11-05: the ripple of the interpolated base, p99 26 m on a mountain tile, is gone; spires above the level stay as outcrops and are counted, `clamped_cells`); the post-pass takes the base closure; `FILL_KERNEL_VERSION` 2; `BakeInputs::weights_at`; the gate example `fill_gate` (the numbers go to the planner: seam pairs, a mountain cube-edge pair, flatness, pit share per class, inland sea-level cells); the owner accepted the u16 0.25 m code.

## Impact
- Code: `generators::bake::spill`, `fillgeom` (new), `tile` and `face` (pass 2's window flood and pass 1's face fill removed), `cache::layer_fill`, `layer_fillpass` (new), `layer_tiles`, `layer_store`, `key` (kind 18), `bake_layer`. Tests: `generators/tests/bake_spill.rs`, `bake_fillgeom.rs`, `bake_tile.rs`, `cache/tests/layer_fill.rs`.
- Known limits of the flat floor: it is flat in the bake's frame (`BakeInputs::base` plus the stored change). The runtime draws the stored bake's C1 lattice read plus the crease detail and fades the change within 3 m of the sea level (`erosion.rs`, `COAST_FADE_M`), so a floor at runtime also carries the lattice read's error (3.4 m rms, p95 5.5 m on 2/12/10, learnings 2026-11-04) and the detail; the cells within 2 cells of a cube edge are cross-faded. A basin's pass-2 mask can differ from its exact surface by the pass-2 base's interpolation error: a shore cell can be left a dimple (not filled) and a spire can stand above the floor (counted, `clamped_cells`).
- The Tracked item of PR 1 (kind 17 tile-local) is removed here.

## Design note: the level of a basin is recoverable (for lake water, F8)
- At any filled cell the bake's level is `BakeInputs::base(direction) + stored change + depth` (flat over a basin to the code's step of 0.25 m, plus the change's own 0.125 m quantisation); no re-bake is needed to read it.
- A lake renderer evaluates the exact base at one cell per connected filled region when it builds the tile and draws the water as its own flat surface at that level (the viewer must hold the class source, which it does with `--class-source`).
- A basin split across tiles gets the same level on both sides: the post-pass is planet-wide (GEN-121).
- Limits: the level is in the bake's frame (the runtime terrain differs by the lattice read, 3.4 m rms on mountains, so a lake edge meets the drawn terrain with that mismatch); a region separated by outcrops (depth 0 cells) is several connected regions of one basin with one level; a cell clamped to depth 0 carries no level; depth saturates at 16,383.75 m.
