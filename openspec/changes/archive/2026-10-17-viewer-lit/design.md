## Design

**Decisions in GPU-free code (CON-03).** `Command::ToggleLit`, `ViewerState::{lit, take_exposure_reset}`, `viewer_lit_plan`, `viewer_pre_ev100`, `lit_warm_plan`, `ExposureReading`, `exposure_text`, `mode_label` and `smoke_verdict` live in `planet_frame::viewer` with tier-A tests. `window.rs` maps keys, calls them and moves data.

**The plan.** The pipelined loop decides the terrain plan; the window clones it and puts the lit plan on it when the lit path is shown (the nodes are copied per frame, a few hundred records). The lit plan is `LitPlan::adaptive_with(look, pre_ev100, dt)` plus `with_atmosphere(look, radius)`, so the sun, tone curve, units, histogram, albedo and atmosphere are the look's. A pure function of (look, radius, dt, reset): the exposure a run reaches is decided by the plans alone (CON-04); `dt` is the frame clock's, capped at 0.25 s like the free camera's.

**Pre-exposure.** ADR 0048 point 3 leaves the viewer's `pre_ev100` to the view ("later from the sun and the altitude"). The viewer uses 12 stops moved by the stops its sun differs from Earth's (`log2(lux / 126,600)`): f16 then holds about +-12 stops around EV 12, which covers a sunlit scene (EV 14 to 15) and dim shadows. A fixed constant is easy to reverse; deriving it from the sun and altitude is item 8's or a later change's (not an ADR: nothing is stored or keyed by it).

**Exposure reset.** The first lit frame snaps the exposure to its target (the oracle's way); so does the first frame after L returns to the lit path and after a restart of the flight (a cut). The flight's loop (last camera to first) is also a cut but is left to the adaptation speeds.

**Exposure in the title.** `LitPath::read_exposure` waits for the GPU; the window calls it where the title is updated (500 ms; 100 ms in the smoke) and, in the smoke, every eighth frame so a short run takes readings by frame count, not by clock.

**Warm-up.** `warm_up_lit(plan)` enables the lit path and, for an atmosphere plan, the atmosphere (the static tables are computed in f64 on the CPU there), and draws the plan once per ring set, each waited. `FirstUse::lit_variants` is 1 once a lit frame has been recorded and 2 once one with the atmosphere has; the tier-C test asserts that measured lit frames with tiles add nothing and create no object. The terrain's lit pipelines are created in `enable_lit`, not drawn in the warm-up (the warm frame has no node, since a node needs a resident tile): the driver's first-draw cost of those two pipelines stays in frame 0, which is a measured frame by ADR 0032.

**Smoke judged on counts.** `SmokeCounts` is kept every run and `smoke_verdict` judges it at the end of the smoke: at least 16 lit frames, all with the atmosphere (when it was wanted), one lit and one debug frame presented, an exposure reading, and the renderer's own account (`executed_passes` of the last lit frame holds `exposure-histogram`, `exposure-adapt` and, with the atmosphere, `sky-view`). The key script: V on the lit path (no effect), L (debug), V (a debug view), L (lit), then vsync, pause, free camera and restart as before.

**Software adapter.** The compact tile format with the atmosphere removes the WARP device (`DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_REMOVED`) when a buffer is copied and mapped after a frame; reproduced in a renderer-level loop (compact pools, atmosphere, `read_exposure` after each frame), not with the vertex format and not without the atmosphere, and the discrete GPU (Vulkan) is unaffected. The software smoke therefore runs in the vertex format (the reference format) unless `--tile-format` says otherwise; the compact format's lit atmosphere runs in the discrete-GPU smoke. Recorded in `docs/learnings.md` and as an open point; it is not diagnosed (no repro in the tier-C atmosphere tests, which wait on `render` and read textures, not buffers).

**Atmosphere that does not fit.** A look whose atmosphere is invalid for the world's radius leaves the lit path without the atmosphere and the run says why (`atmosphere off: ...`); the title then reads `lit`.
