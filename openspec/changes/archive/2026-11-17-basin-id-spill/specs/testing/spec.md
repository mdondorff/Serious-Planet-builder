The fixture's graph is built by hand in the contract's types: it SHALL validate, round-trip, give the same bytes on any thread count, and run under both partition layouts.

Verify: A
Status: active
Source: Lead 2026-11-16 (crusader #155), the river-graph plan's G0 contracts (agreed, owner 2026-11-16) sections 8 and 11
Detail: injected, each found: a cycle; a duplicate id, a kind changed under a kept id, and a basin's spill moved under a kept id (a parent on its deepest child's floor has its own id, no spill cell is not cell 0, a basin with a spill of +inf validates, and a NaN or -inf spill or a NaN floor is not finite); two nodes on one anchor, two junctions on one vertex, a marker at an inflow's end on a node's anchor (a junction at vertex 0 and an exception beside a junction pass); a marker off its reach, out of range, on another vertex, a marker kind without and a non-marker with the field; an unresolved endpoint; a chain without an outlet, followed through a junction; a confluence with one inflow, two inflows into a tile crossing; a node with zero or two outgoing reaches; an edge from an outlet, from a marker, into a source, into an exception marker, at another level at either end; a chain not 8-connected, not starting or ending at its nodes' anchors, an empty reach (reported, no panic); a tributary leaving its tile core (a trunk image passes); a bed rise (passing at an exception marker), a bed not shared at a confluence, and a tributary off the trunk's bed at its junction; `a_geo`, `a_run`, `q` and `q_w` each falling along a reach; a confluence below its inflows, `a_run` and `q_w` not shared there; a NaN; a cell owned twice; a lowering overrun reported once and never excused, a fill overrun excused only by its residual, a raise in KS, a clamp without its residual, a residual whose feature is not flagged, a residual with the clamp's or the fill's numbers wrong and one where nothing clamped, NaN and short inputs.
