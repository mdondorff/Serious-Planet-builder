## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: GEN-176 K7 river beds stay on or above the sea level and the channel cells are fixed after the burn
K7 SHALL burn every land cell of a channel line (a cell standing at or above the sea level when it is burnt) to at least the sea level: the level it is burnt to is `max(bed, sea_level)`, applied after the 0.5 m code of the bed has rounded down; a cell burnt to the floor stays at it, and the cells of the line's sea outlet (below the sea level) are burnt to their bed as before. The channel cells of a window (the cells of the lines in it and the cells the burn lowered) SHALL be fixed for every stage after the burn: the incising carve's breach SHALL NOT lower them and its sweeps SHALL NOT move them; thermal, the deposition sweep and pit spreading SHALL treat them as boundaries (a fixed cell neither gives nor takes). The material that thermal and the deposition sweep would have put on a fixed cell SHALL leave the tile as exported sediment, counted (`thermal_exported_m3`, `deposit_exported_to_fixed_m3`, with `fixed_cells`, in the result and the MARK line) (thermal's is a report only: the givers' loss is already the deposition sweep's eroded column; the sweep's own export to fixed cells is part of its ledger, so G-SED closes); a pit spreads from its free neighbours only, keeping the volume. The final channel pass (GEN-169) SHALL be the only stage after the burn that changes a channel cell, only downward. The reports SHALL count every bound: the final pass's `cutoff_binds` counts every path cell of the window, sub-sea cells included (on the cells' own heights), and the burn reports its own `burn_cutoff_binds` (the cells of the window whose floored target differs from the unbounded carry), both in the MARK line and the G-PROFILE ledger row together with the distribution of what a cutoff costs, the bounded target minus the unbounded one in metres (p50, p99, max); G-PROFILE gates that no line step rises over 0.25 m and that no cutoff changes a height by more than 0.25 m, and a cutoff above it comes to the architect with its tile and cell. A tile with no channel SHALL be bit-identical to the build before this requirement.

#### Scenario: A low coastal plain
- **WHEN** a channel crosses a plain from +20 m down to a sea outlet and its beds run to -10 m
- **THEN** no land cell of the line stands below the sea level and no cell is above its upstream neighbour
