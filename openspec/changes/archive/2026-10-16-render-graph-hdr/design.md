## Design

**Where things live (CON-02, CON-03).** `frame::look` holds every formula a shader implements, in f64 with `libm`, and the plan types. `render::graph` is the GPU-free declaration and compile of the lit frame (it uses no wgpu type; it sits in `render` because ADR 0042 says so). `render::lit` executes: it creates objects once and records passes in the compiled order. The renderer makes no decision: the exposure mode, `pre_ev100`, `dt`, the speeds, the clamps, the sun and the reset all arrive in the plan.

**The frame shape.** `lit_frame_graph(LitShape { size, adaptive })`: `terrain` (writes `hdr`, `depth`), `exposure-histogram` (reads `hdr`, writes `histogram`), `exposure-adapt` (reads `histogram` only when adaptive; writes the persistent `exposure-state`), `tonemap` (reads `hdr` and `exposure-state`, writes the external `presentable`, a side effect). The compile culls the histogram pass under fixed exposure, orders the rest, computes lifetimes and alias groups, and counts bytes (`hdr` 8 B per pixel, `depth` 4, `histogram` 256, `exposure-state` 16). `LitPath` caches the compiled graph with its shape and recompiles when the size or the exposure mode changes; the executor walks `compiled.order` by pass name (the terrain pass is recorded by the terrain renderer into `hdr`). Later passes (atmosphere LUTs, sky, water) are added to this declaration by their own changes.

**Pre-exposure and exposure.** Written value = radiance (units of the sun's illuminance) x 126,600 x `1 / (1.2 x 2^pre)`, clamped to [0, 60000] in the terrain shader. The histogram bins `log2(8 y) + log2(1.2) + pre` of the written luminance `y`; the adaptation (one invocation) reduces 64 bins to the band mean, clamps, steps, and stores `(ev, target, 2^(pre - ev))`; the tone map multiplies the written value by the factor, applies the ACES fit and the sRGB transfer, and writes `Rgba8Unorm` (the encode is in the shader, so the window and the goldens encode identically). Fixed exposure writes `ev = target = fixed`, factor 1. The first lit frame seeds the history at the pre-exposure and snaps. The parameter block and the history buffer are single buffers: the queue orders a `write_buffer` after earlier submissions and before the next, so frames in flight read their own values.

**Terrain shading.** `fs_lit` in the terrain module (shared with the compact pipeline by the existing splice) calls `lit_radiance(normal, height, range, sun, scale)`, a pure function that a tier-B probe compiles into a compute entry to compare with `look::lit_terrain_radiance`. Sun and scale travel in the globals' unused words, so `GLOBALS_BYTES`, the node-record accounting and every pinned size are unchanged. The debug pipelines are untouched: lit pipelines are separate objects made by `enable_lit`, outside `warm_up`; the counts after `enable_lit` are fixed (REND-017 style test).

**Debug view (CON-18).** The tone-map pass has a false-colour mode: whole stops of the exposed luminance below saturation, twelve bands, colours from `look::EV_BAND_COLORS` spliced into the shader. The histogram overlay of ADR 0038 point 5 is not built here (it needs the text or UI path); noted for the planner.

**Goldens.** Three lit frames and the EV-band view at a fixed EV100 15, class `lit`, exact per adapter; they are first goldens, so `golden-review` escalates them and `pending.txt` lists them until `/bless`.
