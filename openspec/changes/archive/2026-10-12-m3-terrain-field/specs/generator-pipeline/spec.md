## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: GEN-016 The terrain field
The terrain field SHALL be a pure function of the unit direction (ADR 0028): the global bake's elevation read at the direction (`read_direction`: face and face coordinates from the direction, bilinear blend of the lattice cell in floating point) plus detail noise in the direction whose amplitude is a nondecreasing function of that elevation, at most `DETAIL_MAX_M`. Every consumer SHALL call `height(dir)`, so the same world point has the same height bits from any tile, parent or child, neighbour across a face edge or cube corner, thread or consumer (COORD-007). Its `height_bounds` SHALL contain the height (also rounded to `f32`) of every direction of the cap, by the face-coordinate Lipschitz bound of ADR 0028 point 3 (faces scanned where the centre's extended coordinates are within `1 + L theta`; the global range for larger caps), with `1e-6 m + 2.4e-7 |anchor|` added to the slope spread, and SHALL hug the terrain on small caps. The detail amplitude is a roughness, not a land decision; the GEN-015 land rule is left to the first consumer that needs land. A read failure of the bake SHALL give NaN from `height` and the global range from `height_bounds`, and be reported by `error()`. Its keys SHALL chain the bake: `inputs_hash` covers the bake's stage key and the persistent height key carries it as `source` (`HeightField::source_key`). The implementation id (`cpu-terrain-v1`) and `TERRAIN_VERSION` SHALL be pinned with known answers, and planning at cells 32 and viewport / tau 240 along the standard flight SHALL not be rejected for steepness (ADR 0016).

Also modified: GEN-015 is limited to readers of tile arrays (`read_direction` is the second reader).
