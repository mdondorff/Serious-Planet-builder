## Why
M5 item 8, first slice (status.md item 8, M4 window lessons, ADR 0020, ADR 0034, ADR 0036). The M4 windows lost scenes to load the harness could not name: ADR 0020's gate looks at the machine for 10 s before a run and nothing looked during one. Window 6 (the first M5 window) will run the lit scenes for minutes each; the architect must be able to read, per scene, how loaded the machine was and by what. The coordinator also asked (opus-planner, 2026-10-17) that a regenerated 16384 store can be checked before a window is requested, so that a bad pack, a missing ocean channel or an incomplete pre-fill fails in a minute and not inside the window.

## What Changes
- `planet_perf::load` (TEST-023): a sampler beside every perf case reads the per-process CPU time of the whole machine every 5 s (`Win32_PerfRawData_PerfProc_Process`, cumulative 100 ns, locale-independent) and turns each pair of readings into the machine's busy share, the harness's own share (its process and its helpers) and the competing share, with the top processes by name. Recorded in the log per scene or view as `load` (aggregates, start and end samples, the series, the top over the case) and as `load_notes`; printed as one `host load` line and `LOAD NOTE` lines. **Recorded only:** nothing voids a verdict. A note appears when the competing load averages more than ADR 0020's 15 % or a process from ADR 0020's busy list used 1 % or more in a sample.
- `perf stream --smoke --store DIR` and `test-render --perf-smoke --store DIR` (TEST-024): the smoke run over a store first prints a read-only store check (GLOBAL_VERSION and MESH_VERSION, the ocean channel read on a fixed grid of 288 directions, every tile of levels 0-L of the tile pack read and decoded in the compact and the vertex form) and fails before any scene when the ocean channel is missing or degenerate or the compact pack is incomplete. It writes nothing to the store (a tier-C test lists the store before and after).
- Wired into the terrain scene (`perf_terrain`, per view) and the streaming scenes (per scene). No existing log field changes; a log without `load` reads and compares as before.

## Impact
Specs: TEST-023 and TEST-024 added. Constitution and ADRs untouched. The note thresholds are ADR 0020's numbers; whether a note should ever void a session is left to the architect after the first window that shows one. No golden, no MESH_VERSION or GLOBAL_VERSION change. Cost during a case: one PowerShell query of about 1.7 s every 5 s on its own thread (the harness's helpers are counted as the harness's load, not as a competitor).
