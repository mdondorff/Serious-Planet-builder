## Why

M3 task 3, first slice: the global-coarse stage needs a field (continents, elevation, climate) and a bake before any stage output can be keyed, stored or read by regional fields. Briefing: new implementation id, bit-exact across runs, thread counts and OSes (CON-14, libm), data products as PNG, 1 km bake under ten minutes.

## What Changes

- `generators::global`: `sample(seed, dir)` (continentalness from a domain-warped fBm, rounded to its stored `f32` before it decides land and shapes the elevation; elevation from basins and ridged mountain ranges inside range zones, continuous across the coast; temperature from latitude and the lapse rate; precipitation from latitude bands), `bake` onto the six cube-face grids with row-chunked threads (at most 2048 cells per face edge), `grid_hash`, and image products (`product` cube net, `equirect_product`). One field with four channels: under ADR 0022 option 1 a chain is one field.
- GEN-013 added (tier A). GEN-014 added (global bake time, tier C, planned: the ten-minute clause moved out of GEN-013 because CON-20 forbids testing time budgets in tier A). Tests (`generators/tests/global.rs`): thread independence and equality with the pointwise field at every vertex, face-edge agreement (exactly 100 shared vertices), climate and land/sea consistency over 60 000 random directions, resolution refusals, image products (written to `target/review/global-coarse/`), pinned known answers with the recorded version asserted (ADR 0006).
- ADR 0026 (proposed): representation, channels and units, what "1 km" means (16384 cells per face edge: 8192 is 1.2 km), persistence and reading of the bake.
- Review notes: numerics review (land/sea decided from the stored f32, tighter tests) and spec-guardian review (version guard, timing clause) applied. Known gaps, deliberately not in this slice and named in ADR 0026: the field takes a raw seed, not a `GenContext` (CON-06; needs a generator wrapper and a GEN-001 contract test before the bake is keyed), `IMPLEMENTATION_ID` is not yet in a key, `bake` holds the whole grid (the tiled bake and the `generate` command come next), the 1 km timing is an estimate (about 4 us per sample per core, 320 s on 20 threads for 16384 cells per face edge) to be measured by the architect.
- Not here: 128-bit stage keys and storage, the ADR 0021 point 7 contract test (no field reads cells yet), regional heights that read the bake.
