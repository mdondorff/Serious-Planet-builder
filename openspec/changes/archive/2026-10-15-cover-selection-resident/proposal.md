## Why

The fresh-plan lag (change `fresh-plan-lag`, #152) showed that a camera turning faster than the 0.5 degree stale-plan limit draws the cover at levels 3 to 8 where its own plan wants 7 to 13 (up to 10 levels of lag), although the residency set R is fully resident: `cover_selection` culled looser than the plan's selection (no tight cut, no horizon-aware "is it in R"), so a non-resident, out-of-plan child stopped its parent's descent. That blurs the world while mouse-looking and contradicts ADR 0031 ("rotation never fetches and never blurs"). Decision of opus-planner (2026-10-15, owner's delegation): fix the code, not the ADR, and do not put a fresh plan on the frame path. ADR 0032's hard measure is "a tile, root or ancestor, for every visible node", so uncovered nodes now count as blank tiles.

## What Changes

- frame, `lod.rs`: `cover_selection_bounded` is the plan's own selection with "resident" as the condition for descending: the plan's LOD test (distance to the tile's height-bounds volume), the plan's horizon, frustum and tight-cut culling from cached per-tile bounds (`TileBounds`, filled from the `SelectionCache` when R is made; no field evaluation on the frame path), a node descends when all its in-view children are resident, a culled child never stops it, and the leaves are balanced 2:1 as the plan's are (a split with a tile missing is undone). With R resident the cover equals the plan of any camera at that position. `cover_selection` (no bounds) stays as the geometry-only variant.
- frame, `pipeline.rs`: `draw_plan_with` (and `draw_plan` over empty bounds): the newest usable plan is drawn only if it covers the frame's view (`plan_covers_view`: every tile of the frame's own cover is drawn by the plan or filled by finer tiles, nothing coarser, no blank node), otherwise the frame draws the cover. A plan made for exactly the frame's request is unchanged.
- frame, `residency.rs`: `ResidencySet` carries the `TileBounds` of its tiles (made by `residency_set_field` from the plan's cache).
- perf/app: nodes of the fresh selection with nothing drawn at, above or below them are blank tiles: the `uncovered nodes` verdict is OVER in every scene but `dive-fast` (report-only), with the first frame and tile ids, and the run exits non-zero with `BLANK TILE` lines after the logs are written. The lag accounting (level + 1) stays.
- specs: STRM-002, STRM-016, LOD-007, TEST-018, TEST-019, TEST-020 modified (no new ids); a learning.

Not in this change: a fresh plan on the frame path; any change to ADR 0031/0032; goldens (none move: the pipelined image-identity tests are unchanged).
