## Why
M5 item 8, third slice. Item 10 runs the M5 acceptance after window 6; the command that judges the window's logs, the report skeleton it writes and the flight videos the owner's look review needs (ADR 0037, status item 10) are built before the window so that the window's logs are judged by code that was tested on synthetic logs, not written afterwards around the numbers.

## What Changes
- `planet_perf::accept_m5` (TEST-026) and `cargo xtask accept M5 [--logs DIR]`: judges the logs of `cargo xtask perf m5` (`lit.json`, `lit-formats.json`, `<scene>-lit.json` with `<scene>-lit.frames.txt`) or refuses; writes `docs/milestones/M5-report.md` (or `target/review/M5-report.generated.md` when it exists or a step failed) with the verdict, the gated rows (five held views against the owner's atmosphere 1 ms, water 2 ms, terrain pass 8 ms and the waited p99 of 20 ms; the five streaming scenes on the lit path through M4's own verdicts), the report-only rows (flights with both shore-filter settings, compact against vertex, GPU span, dive-fast and dive-cold, host load), the facts, and a TO FILL slot for every finding no log carries. The outcome is PASS, FAIL or INCOMPLETE (no gated row failed but a GPU row could not be judged: a view below the clock floor, an idle GPU start, no water in a coast view); only PASS exits zero. A log that cannot be trusted (invalid session, smoke, another size or source, vertex tiles, fewer than three runs, a partial log, an old format, a mixed window) is refused with every reason and the commands.
- `read_scene_as` in `planet_perf::accept` reads a streaming scene's lit log (`stream-lit/<scene>`, the lit field) with the checks of M4's reader.
- `cargo xtask capture flights` and `test-render --scene capture-flights` (TEST-027): the two coastal flights as PNG sequences at 1440p with shore_filter 1 and 0 in `target/review/m5-flights/`, and an H.264 video each when `ffmpeg` is on the PATH. A tool beside the harness: no gate, no timestamps, no perf log; it is not run during a perf window.

## Impact
Specs: TEST-026 and TEST-027 added. No M4 behaviour changes (`accept M4` reads what it read). `docs/milestones/M5-report.md` is not created by this change: it is written by item 10 from a real window.
