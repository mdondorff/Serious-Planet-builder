## MODIFIED Requirements
### Requirement: GEN-189 The canonical river graph
The bake SHALL describe rivers as one canonical graph over two levels (`bake::river_graph`): nodes, reaches and basins, owned by domains, identified by hashes of their defining fields, serialised canonically, and checked by validators that report and never repair. The graph is data and checks only. The algorithms that build it (KS, KG, KV, KC) and the determinism, partition and edit tests of whole builds are later requirements; G0 checks them on the hand-built fixture graph (TEST-140).

Detail:

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Levels | H1 (814 m global cells: trunk reaches from 75 km2, H1 basins) and L (101.8 m global cells: trunk images, tributaries down to `A_crit`, L basins) |
| Domains | `landmass` (H1, every trunk reach at both levels, H1 basins and their L images) or `tile(face, ti, tj)` of K7's canonical grid (GEN-182); a region, window, store or halo is never a domain and never enters an id or a stored value |
| Node | `id`, `kind`, `level`, `anchor` (global cell id at its level), `parent` (the H1 node of an L trunk image), `owner`; a marker also has `on_reach` and `at_vertex`, and its anchor is that reach's vertex |
| Node kinds | `source`, `confluence`, `trib_junction` (marker), `exception` (marker), `tile_crossing`, `outlet_sea` (the first sea cell), `lake_inlet` (enters a basin), `lake_spill` |
| Reach | `id`, `level`, `up`, `down`, `vertices` (an 8-connected chain of global cell ids from `up`'s anchor to `down`'s, both included), `parent` (the H1 reach of an L trunk image), `owner`, per-vertex attributes, `exceptions` (a list of `(reason, value)`, empty for an ordinary reach, at most one per reason; reasons `rise_over_budget`, `unresolved_endpoint`, `budget_residual`, the last one's value the metres of the need not done); reported, never repaired |
| Trunk images | an L trunk node's anchor is the lowest L cell of `S0` within one H1 cell of its H1 anchor, ties by the global cell id (on `S0` only); an L `outlet_sea` image is the first sea cell of its L chain; `parent` the H1 node or reach; images are 1:1; an H1 basin has at most one L image, the L basin holding its floor cell, `parent` the H1 basin |
| Attributes | per vertex, f64: `a_geo` (geometric contributing area, m2), `a_ext` (area entering at a window-entry vertex from outside the tile window: H1's `A_d` of its H1 cell minus the window's own area draining there; zero elsewhere), `a_run` with and without `a_ext` (runoff-weighted area), `q` (potential discharge, m3/s), `q_w` (routed discharge, `q` with the basins' losses); `strahler` (changes only at nodes and `trib_junction` vertices by the standard rule; a reach's order is its last vertex's; every `source` is 1); `bed` (codes of 0.5 m, GEN-170); `width` is derived from `q_w` (GEN-165), never stored |
| Basin | `id`, `level`, `parent` (the larger basin it merges into at its spill), floor (cell, height), spill (cell, height (+inf for a basin with no spill cell; otherwise finite, as the floor), receiving node: a `trib_junction`, a `lake_inlet`, or an `outlet_sea` on the first sea cell of a spill path that reaches the sea before any reach), outlet `active` or `endorheic`, represented `protected_surface` (every kept basin) or `none` (reported depressions), `owner`; kept: H1's set with their L images, and the L basins of the conditioned surface KS1 from 0.25 km2 and 2 m (`S0`'s basins are a diagnostic); KS, KV and KC never change a cell of a protected basin (its ground stays `S0`) |
| Ids | node `hash(body, kind, level, anchor)`; a marker `hash(node hash, on_reach, 0x8A2E_0008)` (`marker_id`, reading 15), so markers are unique per (reach, anchor) and a marker takes its reach's new id; reach `hash(body, level, up, down)` (the contract's order); basin `hash(body, level, floor anchor, spill anchor)` (the spill cell plus one; 0 only for a basin with no spill cell, an endorheic one hashes its spill cell too), the floor's ties by the global cell id; GEN-145's mixer; ownership never enters an id |
| Topology and attributes | a topology change adds or removes an id: a kind change on an anchor, a new `up` or `down`, a moved `trib_junction` cell, a confluence added (two new reaches) or removed (one); trunk reaches split only at H1 events; an attribute change keeps the ids |
| Uniqueness | one non-marker node per (level, anchor); markers of one kind once per (level, anchor, reach), and tributaries ending at one (level, anchor) end at one `trib_junction`, whatever reach it sits on; a `trib_junction` never sits on its reach's last vertex; a marker shares an anchor with a non-marker only as the `up` node of the reach it sits on, at vertex 0 (a tributary continuing above a trunk's head) |
| Degrees | every node but an outlet or a marker has exactly one outgoing reach; only a confluence has two or more incoming reaches; every chain ends in one outlet, followed through a marker along its reach; the graph is acyclic |
| Tile crossings | a tributary leaving a tile core is cut at a `tile_crossing` on its last vertex inside that core, so it lies in one core plus its first vertex; trunk reaches are never cut at tiles; a mismatch between two tiles' crossings is `unresolved_endpoint`; a reach across a cube-face edge is gated at H1 and reported at L |
| Bed | non-increasing on codes along a reach except at an `exception` marker's vertex, across a `lake_spill` (between reaches), and, by reading 41, a rise into the reach's own `down` node (its last vertex) of at most its `budget_residual` value (the coded rise times 0.5 m, in metres); one value at a node, shared by the reaches there; a tributary ending at a `trib_junction` carries the trunk's bed at `at_vertex` |
| Conservation (graph) | `a_geo`, `a_run`, `q`, `q_w` never fall along a reach; at a node every reach carries one shared value; at a confluence it is at least the sum of the inflows one vertex upstream (1e-9 relative, inflows by id); the exact per-cell rule stays GEN-145's |
| Rasterisation (L) | a cell belongs to one reach: a junction cell (a reach's last vertex) to the downstream reach; otherwise the larger `q_w` (-0.0 and +0.0 equal), ties by the lower reach id; per cell `(reach id, vertex)` or none, derived, never authority |
| Budget | `relief_1km(c)`: the range of `S0` over the L cells within 5 (Chebyshev) of the 814 m cell `c`'s centre L cell; `relief10(x)`: the p50 of `relief_1km` over the 814 m cells within `R_relief` (25 km) of `x`'s parent; `budget(x) = min(50, max(10, 0.5 relief10(x)))` m against `S0` |
| Budget use | per stage in order KS (`S0 - KS1`; KS0 is reported, not a stage), KV, KC: the lowering `max(0, in - out)` within the remaining budget, an overrun never excused; KS never raises; the fills of KV and KC each at most `budget`, an overrun a residual; every stage states its wanted lowering and its wanted fill (finite, not negative; a stage that may not fill wants none); a lowering held under its wanted lowering (a clamp) or a fill held under its wanted fill (a fill clamp) writes a residual `(cell, stage, needed, available, feature)` with the stage's numbers (a clamp: the wanted lowering and the budget left before the stage; a fill overrun: the fill and the budget; a fill clamp: the wanted fill and the budget; within 1e-9 m), and the feature's exceptions hold `budget_residual`; residuals and events are one to one: each clamp, fill clamp and fill overrun of a cell and a stage that ran has its own residual, and each residual records one of them; a bed cut is at most `budget - KS_used`, a larger rise adds `rise_over_budget` to the reach's exceptions; checked on f64; the per-stage diagnostic field is u16 `floor(used / 0.1 m)`, never the gate |
| Divide buffer (KV) | KV's weight is zero within `d_buf` (200 m) of a divide cell only where that cell lies farther than `W_floor(q_w)/2 + d_buf` from every graph line (`W_floor` = 3 x GEN-165's width of the nearest vertex's `q_w`); nearer, the catchment boundary meets the river and is valley floor; G1f reports the share of divide cells exempted; not yet checked: KV is a later requirement and holds this row's test |
| Canonical form | nodes, reaches, basins sorted by id; fixed field order; a reach's exceptions sorted by the reason's name, then the value's bits, a signed zero written as +0.0 (`-` for none); header `river-graph 2`; hex as 16 lowercase digits; f64 as IEEE-754 bit patterns in hex; parsed back to the same graph; the parser refuses a value it never writes |
| Diff | ids added or removed (topology), apart from the same id with other fields (attribute); each named by (id, field) |
| Slices | a slice holds the items its domains own; an id it names but does not hold is `missing owner` (a store fact, reported, not a violation) |
| Validators | a cycle; a duplicate id; an id not the hash of its fields; two nodes on one anchor (markers of one kind: on one reach); an unresolved endpoint; a chain without an outlet; a confluence with one inflow, or two inflows elsewhere; a node with zero or two outgoing reaches; an invalid edge (`up` an outlet or a marker, `down` a source or an exception marker, ends at different levels, not an 8-connected chain from `up`'s anchor to `down`'s, attributes not one per vertex); a marker off its reach, or a junction on its reach's last vertex; a tributary leaving its tile core; a bed rise outside the allowed nodes (a rise into a reach's `down` node larger than its `budget_residual`, or without one, is not allowed); a bed not shared; a conservation break; a value not finite (a reach attribute, an exception value, a basin's floor or a spill other than +inf); two reaches owning one cell; a budget overrun; a reason listed twice on one reach; a raise in a lowering stage; a clamp or a fill clamp without its residual; a residual whose numbers are not its stage's, that records nothing, that names no cell or stage that ran, or that is one more than its events; a residual whose feature is not flagged |

#### Scenario: Validators report, never repair
- **WHEN** a graph breaks one of the rules above
- **THEN** the validator returns that violation, names its id, and leaves the graph unchanged

#### Scenario: A rise into a down node within its residual
- **WHEN** a reach's bed rises into its own `down` node by no more than the value of its `budget_residual`
- **THEN** the validators report no bed rise there; a larger rise, a rise at another vertex, or a reach without `budget_residual` is a bed rise
