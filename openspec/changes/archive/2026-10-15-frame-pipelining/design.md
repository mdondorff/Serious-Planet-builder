## Decisions

- **The loop's one wait is the bound.** The loop submits a frame and returns; `FramesInFlight` counts submitted minus finished (the renderer counts finished frames with `on_submitted_work_done` callbacks, run when the device is polled without blocking). When `MAX_FRAMES_IN_FLIGHT` (2, a constant) frames are in flight the loop waits for the oldest (`wait_oldest`) and counts the wait. Slots released by the renderer are reusable `MAX_FRAMES_IN_FLIGHT` frames later (`with_frames_in_flight` hands the bound to the pool's `retire_frames`), the same rule the residency manager's pools use (STRM-006/007): a frame submitted after the release of a slot is at least the bound later, so the frames that may read the slot have retired.
- **A plan is a pure function of its request.** `PlanWorker` runs one plan at a time on a `Spawner`; a request that arrives while one runs waits in a single slot where a newer request replaces it (counted as superseded). Each plan is `plan_terrain_threaded(request)`: the per-tile `SelectionCache` is a memo and the origin heights are evaluated per tile on `threads` scoped threads whose results are keyed by tile, so the plan for a camera does not depend on the thread count, the spawner or when the worker ran (CON-14). Timing decides which cameras get a plan and when it arrives, never what a plan contains.
- **Frame N draws the newest completed plan if usable, else the cover.** `plan_usable`: same frame size, view, grid and lens, the camera within 0.25 of the finest node's split distance of the plan's camera and the view and up directions within 0.5 degrees. A usable plan made for an earlier camera is rebased (origins and matrix of frame N's camera; the nodes are the plan's). Rotation beyond 0.5 degrees makes the plan unusable on purpose: its frustum cut no longer covers the view, so the cover serves until a plan for the new view arrives. A plan made for exactly the frame's request, with every node resident, is returned unchanged: the synchronous frame.
- **Non-resident nodes are replaced by their deepest resident ancestor** (the nearest ancestor with a resident tile; a tile resident under another mesh key counts as not resident). The coarser stand-in wins over any finer drawn tile it covers, so no two drawn tiles overlap. Stand-ins do not morph (interval 1e30 like a root): their geometry is their own. No resident ancestor (not even a root) counts a blank node; with the six roots resident (loaded before the first frame, CON-22) it is zero.
- **The cover is the same rule applied to the view without a plan.** `cover_selection` walks down from the resident roots; a tile splits while the camera is within the level's split distance of the tile (distance from the camera to the tile's resident origin less the tile's radius: the origin is known from the mesh, so no field is read; a bound from the global `max_height_m` was tried first and descends to level 26 over a 9 km disc) and every child in view (horizon and padded frustum test, geometry only) is resident; otherwise the tile itself is drawn. Not balanced (no 2:1 rule): skirts hide the seams. The cover is a pure function of camera, LOD parameters and resident set. At 1 m the camera lies below the coarse surface of a root (ADR 0032 point 5, left open): frame 0 may show that surface from below; the tile check holds, the pixel check of tier B excludes frame 0 of the 1 m teleport.
- **Origin heights on workers, not on the frame.** The whole plan is on the worker; inside the job the origin heights of nodes the cache has not seen are evaluated in parallel (the cost that dominated 7a/7b: 188 distinct bake blocks at 2000 km). The per-tile height bounds of the selection stay serial inside the job; they are not what the frame waits for any more. A field that reads cells (`reads_cells`) keeps the old path.
- **Streaming stays draw-set only** (the bare `Streamer::begin_frame` with the newest completed plan's nodes). The manager needs R from the worker (a second cold evaluation) and slot placement (`upload_at`), and `--stream` must stay equal to the synchronous frame; it is the next change.
- **Columns.** `FrameColumns::NAMES` = plan_ms, scheduler_ms, upload_ms, record_ms, gpu_ms, wall_ms. plan_ms is the worker's time for the plan delivered since the last frame drawn (off the frame path); `largest()` attributes a slow frame among scheduler, upload, record and GPU. A submitted frame has no GPU time; the column is empty in the pipelined loop's log.
- **The harness draws a frame only when its plan changed.** A real loop draws every frame; on a software adapter identical frames only slow the settle.
- **Wall time and scoped threads inside `frame` are deliberate.** `plan_ms` is a report column read from `Instant` inside the worker job; it feeds no decision and tier A never asserts on it (CON-20: time only in perf windows). The origin-height prefill uses scoped threads keyed by tile, so results are independent of scheduling; tier A tests it by thread identity and equality, not by a fake spawner.
