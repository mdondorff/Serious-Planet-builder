## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: TEST-018 Streaming test cases
The streaming cases of ADR 0032 SHALL be camera scripts defined once in a GPU-free crate (`planet_frame::scenes`) and shared by the tier-A fake-clock tests and the perf scenes: one camera per frame of 1/60 s of script time, a pure function of the scene, the height field and a seed, with time entering only as the frame index times the step (CON-04). The dive (`dive`, `dive-fast`) SHALL lower the altitude above the terrain by a constant factor per unit time from 20,000 km to 1 m in 30 s (equal time per decade, 7.3 decades) and then travel north on the ground at 1 m for 10 s at 1.5 m/s (`dive`) or 30 m/s (`dive-fast`), 40 s in all, looking along the direction of travel (pitch a function of time only). The teleport (`teleport-1m`, `teleport-2km`) SHALL hold a view at 2 km until it is settled (the residency set resident, nothing queued, in flight or waiting, for one second) and jump to the antipodal point at 1 m or 2 km altitude for 10 s. Mouse look (`look-1m`, `look-2km`) SHALL hold a position at 1 m or 2 km, settled, for 10 s of seeded yaw and pitch steps with a yaw reversal at every segment and a final 180 degree yaw turn within 0.2 s, from a self-contained integer generator (SplitMix64) identical on every platform. The detail lag of a frame SHALL be judged against the plan selected afresh for that frame's own camera with every tile resident (the plan the synchronous path makes for the camera; the stale-plan rule of the drawing is no part of the definition): the area-weighted mean, over the nodes of that fresh selection, of the node's level (the target) minus the drawn level, weighted by the node's projected pixels (the footprint of its tile edge at its distance from the camera, capped at the screen), where the drawn level of a node is the level of the deepest tile that is resident and drawn in that frame and is the node itself or one of its ancestors; a drawn tile finer than the target is not a negative lag (a node covered only by drawn tiles below it has lag 0); a node with no drawn tile at, above or below it is uncovered, counted as its level plus one levels and reported with its tile id; an uncovered node is a blank tile (ADR 0032's hard measure is a tile, root or ancestor, for every visible node), so zero uncovered nodes in every frame is one of the hard measures below. Every frame of a case has a lag: there is no frame "without a plan" and a gated lag rule is never "not measured" for want of one; a frame whose camera pose or drawn set was not recorded is missing data and fails the case loudly (TEST-020). The hard measures of every case SHALL be: no blank tile in any frame (the draw's own count of nodes with nothing resident, and the uncovered nodes of the frame's fresh selection; the uncovered nodes of `dive-fast` are report-only, like its other criteria), frame median at most 16.6 ms, p99 at most 20 ms and no frame over 40 ms, upload at most 1 MiB per frame and upload time at most 2 ms (judged on the p99 of the frames, the maximum printed), allocated terrain bytes within the tier's share in every frame, terrain-pool VRAM within the tier cap, process peak working set within 8 GiB; the detail-lag budgets SHALL be gated for the dive descent (mean at most 1 level, no frame over 3) and for a still look (lag 0, no request, cancellation or upload) and tracked, not gated, for the teleport (under 1 level within 2 s at 1 m and 1 s at 2 km, draw set complete within 9 s and 4 s) and for the dive's ground segment at walking speed (zero within 2 s), and reported without a budget for the ground segment of `dive-fast`. A budget changes only as a declared change to ADR 0032. The tier-A dive case SHALL model the generation machine, not only the clock: the tile spawner is a generation-rate model (N workers, a duration per job on the fake clock, frames of 1/60 s; `TimedSpawner`) under the default configuration of the pipelined loop (the in-flight limit and the queue ahead derived from the pool size, STRM-020), never a spawner that finishes a fixed number of jobs a frame or a limit above the pool, so that a cap or a refill that waits for the frame boundary shows as lost throughput; the dive case SHALL assert that the tiles generated per second over the frames in which requests wait reach at least 80% of N divided by the job duration (N = 8 and 20, 18 ms a job, the 1,217 tiles/s of ADR 0032 on 20 threads) and that the previous refill, with no queue ahead, does not.

#### Scenario: Dive profile
- **WHEN** the dive script is built
- **THEN** its altitude falls monotonically by the same factor every frame over the first 1,800 frames (4.1 s per decade, 1 m at 30 s), then holds 1 m for 600 frames while the position moves north at the scene's speed, and `dive` and `dive-fast` share the descent exactly

#### Scenario: Teleport script
- **WHEN** a teleport script is built
- **THEN** it has a prelude camera at 2 km, 600 identical frames at the antipodal point at the scene's altitude, and is the same on every build

#### Scenario: Mouse look script
- **WHEN** the look angles are built for a seed
- **THEN** they are 600 frames, identical for the same seed and different for another, with the known first output of the generator, at least eight yaw reversals, no step faster than 180 degrees per second before the turn, pitch within its limits, and the last 12 frames turn the yaw by exactly 180 degrees

#### Scenario: Detail lag
- **WHEN** a node of level 2 is drawn by its level 0 ancestor, another by itself, a third has no drawn tile at, above or below it, and a fourth has only finer tiles drawn below it
- **THEN** the lag is the area-weighted mean of 2, 0, the third's level plus one and 0, the third is reported uncovered with its tile id, a drawn tile finer than the target is not a negative lag, and with a level 1 and a level 0 ancestor both drawn the deepest one counts

#### Scenario: Verdicts
- **WHEN** the recorded frames of a case have a blank node or an uncovered node (outside `dive-fast`, where it is reported), a p99 above 20 ms, a frame over 40 ms, an upload above 1 MiB or 2 ms, allocated bytes above the share, or a gated lag over its budget
- **THEN** the verdict for that measure is OVER and names the frame, the value and the budget; a tracked budget over is reported `over (tracked, not gated)`; a reading that is not available (memory) is `not measured`, never a pass; a frame without a lag is missing data and is OVER for every lag rule

#### Scenario: Mouse look at the settled state
- **WHEN** the managed loop on fakes is settled and the camera only turns, at 0.2, 0.7, 3, 10 and 15 degrees a frame (the last a half turn in 0.2 s), with and without pitch changes
- **THEN** every frame has lag 0 and no uncovered node

#### Scenario: Dive on the generation-rate model
- **WHEN** the dive wants (20,000 km to 1 m, then a hold) run through the streamer on 8 and on 20 model workers of 18 ms a job with the default queue ahead, and again with none
- **THEN** every frame with a request waiting has all workers busy, the generation rate is at least 80% of N / 18 ms, and without the queue ahead it is under 70% of it
