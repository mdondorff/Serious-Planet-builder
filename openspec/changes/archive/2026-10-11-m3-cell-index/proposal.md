## Why

M3 plan and ADR 0007: an edit must invalidate only what it touches. Node reads already do (WDEF-004). Authored items and their spatial queries are the other half: the cell is the invalidation unit, and ADR 0006/0007 require that a query which found nothing is still part of the key.

## What Changes

- `world-def::cells`: cell level per body radius (40 km cells, level 8 at Earth's radius, `libm`), `cell_of`, `cells_covering(tile, level, max)`, `cell_hash` with a fixed hash for the empty cell.
- `WorldDefinition`: authored items `(body, id) -> (content hash, unit direction)` with a cell index derived from them and rebuilt for a body whenever its radius changes (no stranded items), `put_item` (add or move; validates the radius and the direction), `remove_item`, `items_in_cell`; `hash()` covers items including their directions (and equals the node hash when there are none), `base_hash()` covers the nodes only. `to_toml` refuses a definition with items. Item payloads, ULIDs, layers and TOML item files belong to M6 (proposed ADR 0021).
- `GenContext::query_cell` and `query_tile(body, tile, margin_cells, max_cells)`: record `derived.cell.<body>.<cell>` with the cell hash, empty cells included, plus the radius read; the tile's footprint plus a margin ring (edge and corner neighbours across cube faces); more than `max_cells` cells reads the whole body (`derived.cell.<body>.all`), so the answer is always exact.
- `HeightField::reads_cells()` (default false) and `inputs_hash_for(tile, map)` (default: the field-wide hash): height and mesh keys use it, and `MeshKeyer` computes the field-wide hash once unless the field reads cells, so a field that queries cells keys each tile by what that tile read at no cost to the others. No existing key changed.
- Modified: WDEF-004 (queries), WDEF-005 (now active). Tests: world-def `cell_index` (12), generators `cell_keys` (3: invalidation of height and mesh keys by add, edit, move and remove, the margin, adjacent cells, face edges). Review findings fixed: radius edits re-file items, margin ring, whole-body fallback instead of `None`, per-tile cost, validation, mapping passed in.
- Not here, written down in proposed ADR 0021: item extent (`reach`) and layers in the query, 128-bit ids and content hashes, region summary hashes for coarse tiles, authored stages keyed by upstream keys (CON-08), and a contract test that a generator's recorded reads stay within its declared ones. Also not here: a real stamp generator, loading items from files.
