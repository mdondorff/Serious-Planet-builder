## Why
ADR 0052 (golden-review implements it since #236) lets the debug views of first M5 goldens agree across adapters with at most 64 pixels beyond delta 1 plus at most 32 at delta 1. The 18 pending M5 goldens (8 lit, 6 sky, 4 water) are reviewed again with the tool; the declarations below are copied from the proposals that produced them (sun-and-base-shading, atmosphere-passes, water-pass).

## Outcome
Reviewed with `cargo xtask golden-review` (ADR 0052 as implemented in #236: at most 64 px beyond delta 1 plus at most 32 px at delta 1 per adapter pair) over CI main run 39029009545 (WARP .33438, llvmpipe) and the local RTX run, with this run's JUnit (`cargo xtask test`, `cargo xtask test gpu --real`). Blessed AUTO-M5 on all three adapters: lit_orbit_ev_bands and the six sky goldens. The other 11 escalate on the delta-1 cap; counts are in `docs/status.md`. The water-pass proposal named its bound tests as `gpu_water::name`, which the tool matches only as a bare name or the full `crate::binary::name`; they are written as bare names here (same tests).
