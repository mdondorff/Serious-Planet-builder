## Decisions

- **Where**: the budget is applied where `upload_at` is called, in `ManagedLoop::begin_frame`, so the renderer and the pool are untouched. The streamer already counts a finished tile as resident (its slot is taken); the sink holds it only when the loop has written it. The invariant of the tests: streamer resident = sink + pending.
- **Order**: `(class, level, plan order, tile)`, a total order, so the result does not depend on the order the jobs finished in (CON-14). Classes come from the wants the frame started with: D with its ancestors and the six roots, R with its ancestors, the prefetch set, other. A tile of D class is ranked by the best (lowest) plan index among the draw tiles it is an ancestor of.
- **Strict prefix**: the first tile that does not fit stops the frame. Letting a smaller tile behind it through would reorder across the order the classes define; the cost is a little unused budget. The one-tile rule (the first tile is always written) means a tile larger than the cap goes alone and cannot starve.
- **Sizes**: `TileSink::upload_bytes(&mesh)`; the default is `vertices * MeshVertex::STRIDE`, so fake sinks with vertex-less meshes weigh 0 and are never deferred (the existing tests are unchanged). The compact sink overrides it with `slot_bytes(cells)` (8,840 B at cells 32).
- **Node records**: reported per frame as `indirect_frame_bytes(plan nodes)` (the indirect path's figure). Not deferrable, so they cannot be part of a deferral rule; the option `node_records_in_cap` reduces the tile allowance by the previous drawn plan's node bytes (this frame's plan is made after the uploads).
- **No blank**: a deferred tile is not in the sink, so the pipeline substitutes its deepest resident ancestor or draws the cover (STRM-016). The roots are class D, so they come first; with a cap below six root tiles the first frames would be blank until the one-tile rule has written them (tested), at 1 MiB they are in the first frame.
- **Fill times** (whole tiles per frame): compact 8,840 B gives 118 tiles per frame, so the 8,763 slots of a ground-1m fill take 75 frames (ADR 0033 says 74 by bytes); the vertex format, 53,724 B, gives 19 per frame and 462 frames.
