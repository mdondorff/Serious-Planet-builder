## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: TEST-025 M5 lit perf scenes
The performance harness SHALL provide the scene `lit` (`test-render --perf --scene lit`, `cargo xtask perf lit`) that measures the lit path (the look's sun, the atmosphere, the water pass, adaptive exposure, the tone map) at 1440p over the views ground 1 m, aerial 20 km, coast 2 km, coast 3 m and orbit (the viewer's cameras at 20 N 30 E and two cameras 2 km and 3 m above the water at the first shoreline east of 30 E whose sea reaches 2 km out and land 500 m in, found by `planet_frame::litscenes::find_coast`) and over the two coastal flights (`coast-glide`: 6 m over the water from 400 m out, 228 m towards the shore; `coast-descent`: 300 m to 4 m, log-spaced, 1,200 m out closing to 500 m; 240 cameras each, `--flight-frames N`), the flights once with `water.shore_filter` at the look's default and once at the other setting (`<flight>@filter0` or `@filter1`, for inspection). It SHALL refuse a world without a coast or without the ocean channel, naming the remedy. Each held view SHALL be planned and uploaded once, drawn in waited timed frames (`render(.., true)`) after `enable_lit`, the atmosphere and the water pass were warmed and the exposure seeded at the view (REND-017: no first-use work in a measured frame), brought to the GPU's loaded state (ADR 0034, ADR 0036) and measured in three runs of `--frames` frames with the run-to-run noise rule and its one retry (ADR 0020); each flight camera SHALL be planned, its new tiles uploaded, drawn once untimed and twice timed, in one run. The log of a run SHALL carry, besides the waited frame time, the metrics `cpu_ms`, `gpu_ms` (the terrain pass, as in the terrain scene), `gpu_water_ms`, `gpu_sky_view_ms`, `gpu_aerial_perspective_ms`, `gpu_sky_composite_ms`, `gpu_exposure_histogram_ms`, `gpu_exposure_adapt_ms`, `gpu_tonemap_ms`, `gpu_scene_copy_ms`, the per-frame sums `gpu_atmosphere_ms` (sky view, aerial perspective and composite) and `gpu_water_total_ms` (water pass and scene copy) and `gpu_span_ms` (REND-028), and the counts nodes, draw calls, water nodes and draws, tile format and the lit and atmosphere flags; the static tables' upload has no row (a queue write on a body's load, not a pass of a steady frame) and the console says so. A static view SHALL be judged, when it is valid for GPU figures (ADR 0036), against the owner's budgets of 2026-10-15: the median of the atmosphere sum at most 1 ms, the median of the water sum at most 2 ms (a view with no water node says `not in view`), the terrain pass at most 8 ms and the GPU span at most 16.6 ms, and, at any clock state, the p99 of the waited frame at most 20 ms; a view not valid for GPU figures prints its figures with `NOT VALID for GPU budgets (<state>)` and no verdict; a flight prints the same rows as reported (`over (not gated)` when above) and gates nothing. The console SHALL print per view one row of every pass's median, p99 and maximum, the host load (TEST-023), and for the atmosphere and water sums the run-to-run noise, its absolute spread and whether the proposed floor of ADR 0036 point 3 (also above 0.1 ms) would have kept the view noisy (recorded, not applied). `--views a,b,c` SHALL restrict the views (a partial log is not a window). The scene `lit-formats` SHALL measure ground 1 m, aerial 20 km, coast 2 km and orbit in the vertex format and then in the compact format, view by view, log `lit-formats/<view>/<format>`, print the compact over vertex ratio of the median of the terrain pass, the atmosphere sum, the water sum and the GPU span, and store the ratios in the compact run's counts (`ratio_*_permille`); it gates nothing. The streaming scenes SHALL take `--lit` (`perf stream NAME|all --lit`): the same scripts, the same pipelined loop and the same verdicts of TEST-018 and TEST-019 with each frame drawn lit with the atmosphere and the water of its camera (the lit plan and the water plan inside the timed frame, the lit path warmed before the settle), the log named `<scene>-lit.json` in a log directory, the path `stream-lit/<scene>` and `protocol.field` ending in `; lit path (atmosphere, water)`, so that a lit log is never compared with a debug-path log; GPU time stays not measured in them. A smoke run (`--perf-smoke`, `perf lit --smoke`) SHALL run the same path on any adapter at 640 by 360 for the lit scenes, print the pass rows and no budget rows, run the vertex format on a software adapter and never be a measurement. `cargo xtask perf m5 --store DIR [--out DIR]` SHALL run `lit`, `lit-formats` and the seven streaming scenes with `--lit` in that order, each under the readiness gate and the streaming scenes each in their own process, continue after a failing part and exit non-zero if one failed.

#### Scenario: Budget rows of a static view
- **WHEN** a view valid for GPU figures has an atmosphere sum of exactly 1 ms and a water sum of exactly 2 ms, and another has 1.5 ms and 2.5 ms
- **THEN** the first is within both budgets and the second fails the atmosphere and water rows, the other rows unchanged

#### Scenario: A view below the clock floor
- **WHEN** a view's clock samples read 1,150 MHz in P2 and its waited frame p99 is 31 ms
- **THEN** every GPU row prints its figure with `NOT VALID for GPU budgets (P2 1150 MHz ...)` and no verdict, and the frame row is OVER at that clock state

#### Scenario: Water and atmosphere not in the view, and flights
- **WHEN** a view has no water node, or the atmosphere is not drawn, and a flight is over a budget
- **THEN** the row says `not in view` and fails nothing, and the flight's row says `over (not gated)`

#### Scenario: The coast and the flights
- **WHEN** a world has sea on the western half of a parallel and land on the eastern half
- **THEN** the coast is the first sea-to-land crossing east of 30 E with the heading towards the land, the glide holds 6 m over the water and the descent runs from 300 m to 4 m with any frame count, and a world without a shoreline, or with a pond whose sea does not reach 2 km out, has no coast

#### Scenario: Smoke on the software adapter
- **WHEN** the scenes `lit` and `lit-formats` and a streaming scene with `--lit` run in smoke mode over an in-memory bake
- **THEN** every view and both flights with both shore-filter settings print a pass row and a waited frame time, the log carries the per-pass metrics where the adapter times its passes, the lit scenes print no budget row, the streaming log is `stream-lit/<scene>` with the lit field, and `--views` with an unknown name, `--views` or `--flight-frames` with another scene and `--lit` with the terrain scene are refused
