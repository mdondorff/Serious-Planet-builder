## MODIFIED Requirements
### Requirement: BUILD-055 The river gate prints the river numbers of a region
`cargo xtask river-gate <store dir | window>` SHALL print, in release and under the heavy lock, on one region at L: the share of river cells inside in closed basins (at least 0.5 m deep and 16 cells, `--min-cells`) beside SPIKE-114's 5 cm reference; the valley depth (surface 1 to 2 cells out above the river cell, the canyon a viewer sees) and the burn (surface above the river level, K7's cut), each a share over 50 m; ring 2-4 and the erosion depth; the share in lakes deeper than D (`--d`, default 20 m); p50 relief at 2 x 2 and 10 x 10 cells beside the start's; H1's edge crossings matched within 2 km. It SHALL exit 0.

Verify: A
Status: active
Source: Lead 2026-11-14 (explorer #143, P3 E1 and E2), SPIKE-114, SPIKE-115, crusader 2026-11-14 (surfaces, canyon, N), TEST-136, lead 2026-11-14 (canonical window names), lead 2026-11-14 (DEC crusader-4: the canyon as valley depth and burn), spikes 2026-11-15 (SPIKE-119: windows M1 and F1)
Detail: The valley depth is the p50 of the surface at Chebyshev distance 1 to 2 (102-204 m) above the surface at the river cell; its bar is relative to Earth: at most the share of a GLO-30 tile with 1 to 2 x the window's relief at 10 x 10 cells (printed beside it; the review table gives the reference). The burn is the surface at the river cell above the river level, every basin breached and with the lakes of D kept. Ring 2-4 is the p50 of the surface 2 to 4 cells out above the river level (the valley depth there plus the burn). A window is `face,i0,i1,j0,j1,seed` in regional cells or a canonical name: R1h face 1, 8056-8675 x 760-1379, seed 14 (the E1 region stores' window, the default); R3a face 4, 368-987 x 4720-5339, seed 15; R3b face 2, 9968-10587 x 8304-8923, seed 15; M1 (mountain belt) face 4, 6600-7219 x 10200-10819, seed 15; F1 (low-gradient river landscape) face 4, 4800-5419 x 3300-3919, seed 15. The basins are found under a fill of 1e-7 m a cell. A window measures its start surface (the base's C1 read B plus the regional field) over the region's box. A store measures its stored ground B + L (an L tile is K7's ground minus B) over the commit's tiles, with the start surface around them, its tiles draining to their border, at the commit's sea level; a commit of another grid, radius, seed, base or (with the keys) inputs identity is refused; `--no-key-check` reads each tile under the key its commit names and the report says "keys not checked". An H1 crossing counts with Q of at least 1 m3/s. An unknown option, a missing value, a second window and `--no-key-check` without a store are refused.

#### Scenario: A window
- **WHEN** the gate runs on `r1h`, `r3a`, `r3b`, `m1` or `f1`
- **THEN** it measures that canonical window and prints one line each for rivers, basins, valley depth, burn, ring 2-4, erosion depth, lakes, relief and edge, and exits 0

#### Scenario: A spike store
- **WHEN** the gate runs on a spike branch's store with `--no-key-check`
- **THEN** it names the commit and prints "keys not checked: spike store"

## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: BUILD-057 The river gate's measurement suite
`cargo xtask river-gate` (BUILD-055) SHALL print, after the gate lines and from the same input (a window's start surface or a store's baked ground), one line per metric, each beside the absolute count it is taken over: the gradient, the valley width, the cross-section asymmetry, the drainage density, the catchment ratio at H1 crossings, the seam at the region's edge, the narrowness ratio, the slope-area scaling and the relief power spectrum. The lines SHALL be the same bits for the same input.

Verify: A
Status: active
Source: Lead 2026-11-15 (explorer #151, P3 E1; the owner's rewrite of P3 after the external review), BUILD-055, TEST-137
Detail: One definition per metric. Gradient: a river step is a river cell to its receiver when that is a river cell; it is uphill when the receiver's surface stands more than 0.25 m above it; the count of uphill steps, of steps and the largest rise are printed. Valley width: across the flow (perpendicular to the step to the receiver) the surface is walked in cell steps, bilinear, up to 64 cells to each side, to where it first stands above the river cell's surface by the depth; the width is both sides in metres, the crossing interpolated; the depth is 10 m, and half the cell's ring 2-4 depth (a cell whose ring 2-4 depth is under 2 m has no half-depth width and is counted as flat); p50 and p90 with the number of cells and of sides that reached the limit. Asymmetry: |L - R| / (L + R) where L and R are the mean of the rises above the river cell's surface of the points 2, 3 and 4 cells out on the two sides (cells with L + R at least 1 m; p50, p90, count). Drainage density: the river steps' length (a diagonal step is the cell width times the square root of 2) inside over the land area inside, km/km2. Catchment ratio: each H1 crossing of Q at least 1 m3/s matched (2 km, same direction; the nearest L crossing) gives L's drainage area there over H1's Q; the median of these is the implied runoff, and p10 and p90 of each ratio to that median are printed (1 when L carries H1's catchment area at every node); the pair count is printed, none without an H1 crossing. Seam: the height steps |dz| between 4-neighbours with one cell inside the region's box and one outside (count, p50, p99, maximum), against p50 and p99 of the steps between neighbours both inside. Narrowness: the valley depth (BUILD-055, surface 1 to 2 cells out above the river cell) over the ring 2-4 depth of each river cell whose ring 2-4 depth is at least 1 m (p50, p90, count) and the ratio of the two p50s. Slope-area: per land cell inside with a receiver and a positive slope (surface drop over the step length) and an area of at least 4 cells, binned by half decades of the area in cells from 4; per bin the p50 slope and the count; theta is minus the least-squares slope of log p50 slope against log area over the bins of at least 30 cells (0 with fewer than two). Spectrum: the largest power-of-two square (at most 1024 cells) in the bounding box of the cells inside, centred in it; the mean removed, a Hann window, the 2-D FFT, the power per octave of the radial wavenumber (cycles per square, mean over the octave's bins, m2) from wavenumber 1 on, and beta the least-squares slope of log power against log wavenumber from the second octave on; printed for the surface and for the start. The Earth benchmark that gives the bars is the spikes' (windows, transects and the GLO-30 reference, #121), not this requirement's.

#### Scenario: A store
- **WHEN** the gate runs on a store
- **THEN** the nine suite lines are measured on the stored ground B + L over the commit's tiles, with the same definitions as on a window

#### Scenario: Metrics of a valley
- **WHEN** the surface is a V valley with a river on its floor
- **THEN** the width at 10 m, the asymmetry, the narrowness and the density have the values of its geometry (TEST-137)
