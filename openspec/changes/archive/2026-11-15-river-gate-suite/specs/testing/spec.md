## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: TEST-137 The river gate's measurement suite on fixtures
Tier-A fixtures SHALL check each metric of the suite (BUILD-057) against a fixture whose value follows from its geometry, and that the same input gives the same report.

Verify: A
Status: active
Source: Lead 2026-11-15 (explorer #151), BUILD-057
Detail: a draining valley has no uphill step and a 3 m pit on its river has one; the width at 10 m of a valley with sides of 2 m a cell is 1,000 m, narrower for 4 m a cell and capped on a flat floor; a symmetric valley has asymmetry 0 and sides of 4 and 2 m a cell 1/3; the density is the river length over the land area; an H1 crossing matched at the region's edge gives one catchment pair and ratio 1, a seam of 1 m steps reads 40 pairs against 2 m inside, and a 50 m step at the edge shows in the seam only; edge pairs take the nearest crossing of the direction within 2 km; the narrowness is under 0.7 for a V and over 0.85 for a trench; the slope-area exponent is 0 for a ramp and positive for a profile steeper upstream; a sinusoid of period 8 cells peaks at octave 2 and of 16 cells at octave 1, and a flat surface has no power; one line per metric is printed and the same input gives the same report.
