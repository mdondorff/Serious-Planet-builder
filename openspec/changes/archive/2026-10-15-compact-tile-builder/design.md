## Decisions

- **One key, two formats.** A compact tile's `key` is `mesh_key_field` of the same tile. The format of a tile is a pure function of its level and (at the finest level) its relief, so the key does not need to say which; a plan names a tile and a key, the renderer holds whichever format the builder chose. Consequence: MESH_VERSION is the one version of both formats; the compact construction is covered by it (bumped in the switch-over change, which re-records the known answers).
- **Normal precision measured, not calculated.** ADR 0033 gave about 0.002 degrees for two 16-bit octahedral components "by calculation". Measured over 212,006 directions (random, axes, the fold at z = 0 and 1e-4) the worst is 0.0037 degrees; on tiles of the reference, eroded and steep fields it is 0.0036 degrees. CON-14's 0.01 degrees holds with a factor 2.7. The angle in the tests comes from the cross product: `acos` of a dot product near 1 reads 0.03 degrees from f32 rounding alone.
- **Quantisation range rounding.** `hr_min` is rounded down and `hr_step` up in f32, so the 16-bit range always covers the tile's heights without clamping.
- **The CON-13 guard** looks at the finest level only (`level >= max_level` and `relief / 65,535 > 1 mm`, i.e. more than 65.535 m of relief), as ADR 0033 point 5 states; coarser tiles keep a step above 1 mm where CON-13 does not apply (3.2 mm at level 8 with 65 m of relief) and the tests bound the height error by half a step.
- **Finest level 22 (ADR 0033 point 6) is not part of this change.** It is a LOD parameter (`LodParams::max_level`, 26 everywhere today) that changes plan counts, pinned counts and the goldens of the ground-level views independently of the tile format; ADR 0033 lists it as its own "LOD spec" delta. It stays a separate change so the format's goldens can be judged on their own (a format change must be within rounding; a cap changes which tiles exist).
- **Where the mirror lives.** `shade_vertex` is the CPU twin of the vertex shader in the generators crate (f32, same operations), the oracle for the tier-C comparison of the renderer change and for the tier-B model-error bound here.
- **The format depends on the view's finest level, the key does not.** `CompactTile::allowed_at_finest_level` lets a consumer that reuses a tile across views with different `max_level` re-check the CON-13 guard; no cache stores `TileData` yet (the pack holds height tiles only), so nothing reuses tiles across views today.
- **Seam bound.** The measured same-level mismatch is 4.4 m at level 3, 0.23 m at level 4, 15 mm at level 6, 3.6 mm at level 8 and 0.3 mm at level 12 (ADR 0033's 5.5 mm holds from about level 7); all far under the skirt. Cross-face pairs are not compared by this test.
