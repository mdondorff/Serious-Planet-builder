## Decisions

- **One script definition.** `planet_frame::scenes::build_script(scene, radius, aspect, field, seed)` returns a prelude camera (held, unmeasured, until the view is settled: teleport and look) and one camera per 1/60 s frame. The tier-A tests of this change pin its shape (monotone dive with a constant factor per time and 4.1 s per decade, ground speed, antipodal landing, SplitMix64 known answer, reversals, the 180 degree turn in 12 frames); the fake-clock cases and the perf scenes call the same function. Time enters only as frame index times the step (CON-04).
- **Dive pitch.** ADR 0032 says "a fixed pitch (no look input)". A constant pitch cannot show the planet at 20,000 km (it spans 14 degrees from nadir) and gives a walker a nearly vertical view, so the script uses the TEST-013 flight's law: 85 degrees down tapering to 8 degrees in proportion to the descent time, then 8 degrees on the ground; a function of time only, no look input. The ADR's wording is read as "no look input"; the constants are in one place if the architect wants another. Open point.
- **Pacing.** The managed loop needs real time between frames for its workers, so a frame starts one step after the previous one was due (sleep, then a 2 ms spin); a late frame moves the schedule. The pacing sleep and the lag bookkeeping are outside the frame time. Frame time = the iteration's work: scheduler, upload, record, and a wait for the GPU only at the frames-in-flight bound; the plan is delivered, never waited for (its column is the worker's time).
- **Lag.** Per frame: if the drawn frame is the exact plan, 0; else, when a plan usable for the camera exists (`base_seq`), the area-weighted mean over its nodes of (node level minus the level of the deepest drawn ancestor-or-self), the weight being the node's projected pixels (edge = cells x sample spacing, distance of its origin, capped at the screen). Frames without a usable plan (a cover frame after a jump) have no lag; they are counted and shown ("N frames without a plan to judge"), never counted as zero, and for the teleport's recovery times a frame without lag is "not recovered". Blank: a node with no drawn tile of its own or an ancestor, or an empty drawn plan.
- **Verdicts.** Gated (fail the case): blank tiles, frame median <= 16.6, p99 <= 20, max <= 40, upload bytes <= 1 MiB, upload time p99 <= 2 ms (the p99 is this change's reading of "upload <= 2 ms"), terrain bytes within the share, renderer VRAM within the tier cap, driver VRAM and process peak working set within caps where readable, dive descent lag (mean <= 1, no frame over 3), still look (lag 0, no request, cancellation or upload). Tracked: teleport recovery (under 1 level within 2 s / 1 s, draw set complete within 9 s / 4 s), the dive's ground segment (zero within 2 s). Report only: `dive-fast`'s ground segment. A run fails (non-zero exit, after the logs are written) only for a blank tile; the OVER lines are the architect's to read.
- **Memory readings.** Renderer-allocated VRAM is `pool_allocated_bytes` after every frame (counted, exact). Driver VRAM is `nvidia-smi memory.used` after the case (the whole GPU, all processes). RAM is the process's peak working set over its life (`PeakWorkingSet64` through one PowerShell call, `VmHWM` on Linux), which includes the bake and the store's block cache and, in `streaming`, the earlier scenes; an upper bound. Anything unreadable is "not measured".
- **Source.** `--store DIR` (default 16384 cells, `--global-res` overrides) opens the stored bake with a leaked `TileStore` and `StoreSource` (the field borrows them for the process). Each scene of a `streaming` session opens a fresh reader, so an earlier scene's decoded-block cache does not warm the next scene's cold plans (the OS file cache stays warm). `protocol.source` is `store DIR, N cells` or `in-memory N cells (NOT the production source)`; an in-memory run adds a reason to the session's validity. Without `--store` the scenes refuse unless `--allow-memory-bake` (smoke excepted). Baselines are per scene file (`<scene>.json`, `<scene>.last-valid.json`), and a log is compared only with one of the same source and field.
- **Clock.** Samples before, every 3 s during (a sampler thread, never on the frame path) and after the case; the first sample sits in `clocks_before` and the lowest-clocked one in `clocks_after`, so ADR 0036's rule (every sample at the floor) reads off the existing log fields; `clock_samples` and `clock_min_mhz` are in the counts. No GPU warm-up: frame verdicts hold at any clock state and no GPU verdict is given. Open point: a cold first seconds of a dive on an idle GPU is part of its frame time.
- **`Rig`.** The renderer, managed loop, frames in flight and pool configuration that `run_script` built inline are now `pipelined::Rig`, used by both.
