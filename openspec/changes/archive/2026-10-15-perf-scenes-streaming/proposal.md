## Why

M4 item 7, last sub-item: ADR 0032 (accepted 2026-10-15) defines the dive, teleport and mouse-look cases and their measures; the managed pipelined loop (#132, #133), the upload cap (#142), the compact render path (#137) and the finest level 22 (#147) now exist, so the perf scenes `dive`, `dive-fast`, `teleport-1m`, `teleport-2km`, `look-1m`, `look-2km` can be built for the architect's perf window (`cargo xtask perf`; coders never run it).

## What Changes

- frame (GPU-free): `planet_frame::scenes`: the six scenes as camera scripts, one camera per frame of 1/60 s of script time, a pure function of the scene, the height field and a seed (`dive_altitude_m`: constant factor per time, 20,000 km to 1 m in 30 s, then 10 s on the ground at 1.5 m/s or 30 m/s; the teleport from a settled 2 km view to the antipode at 1 m or 2 km; a seeded SplitMix64 mouse-look sequence of 600 frames with yaw reversals ending in a 180 degree turn in 0.2 s), and the detail-lag measure (`frame_lag`: area-weighted level gap between the plan's nodes and the tiles drawn for them, blank nodes named). The tier-A fake-clock cases and the perf scenes use these functions.
- perf (GPU-free): `planet_perf::streaming`: the per-frame record (plan, scheduler, upload, record, GPU, wall columns, lag, blank nodes, uploaded bytes, resident, in flight, cancelled, degraded, allocated bytes), the rules and budgets of ADR 0032 point 4 per case (detail lag gated for the dive descent and mouse look, tracked for the teleport and the dive's ground segment, report-only for `dive-fast`'s), the verdict lines, the memory readings (process peak working set, driver VRAM) with "not measured" where unreadable. `Protocol.source` / `protocol.source` in the log beside `protocol.field`; the regression report compares a log only with one of the same scene (each scene has its own log and baseline files) and the same source; `detail_lag` is never compared as milliseconds.
- app: `streamperf.rs` (`perf_streaming`): `test-render --perf --scene dive|dive-fast|teleport-1m|teleport-2km|look-1m|look-2km|streaming --store DIR` runs the pipelined managed loop on the real renderer (`pipelined::Rig`, extracted from `run_script`), paced at 60 Hz by the real clock (the clock only paces and measures; the script is a function of the frame index), writes `<scene>.json`, `<scene>.frames.txt` and the baseline, prints the verdicts, and fails after writing if any frame drew a blank tile (frame number and tile ids). `WorldTerrain::open_store` reads a stored bake (the production 16384-cell source); without `--store` the scenes refuse unless `--allow-memory-bake` (labelled `NOT the production source`, session not valid for streaming budgets). `--perf-smoke --scene ...` proves the scenes on the software adapter. `MeshSource` takes the tile size (cells) from the request (it was the constant 16, so the pipelined loop could not run the production 32).
- xtask: `cargo xtask perf stream [SCENE|all] --store DIR [--allow-memory-bake] [--global-res N] [--out PATH] [--machine-ready] [--smoke]`.
- Specs: new TEST-018 (the streaming test cases: scripts, measures, detail lag; tier A) and TEST-019 (the streaming perf scenes; tier C). TEST-012 is untouched; the scenes cite it for the columns it already defines.

## Not in this change

- No perf measurement (the architect's window), no budget change, no golden.
- The fake-clock cases of ADR 0032 point 8 tier A (fake I/O with 200 ms latency, a generation-rate model; asserts on requests, cancellations and fill order) and the tier-B renders of the scripts are separate queue items; this change gives them the scripts and the lag measure.
- GPU time per frame: the pipelined submit has no timestamp readback without a wait, so the `gpu_ms` column is reported as not measured and the scenes give no GPU verdict (frame verdicts hold at any clock state, ADR 0034 as amended).
