## MODIFIED Requirements
### Requirement: REND-034 Procedural ground detail
The lit terrain shader SHALL add a procedural detail term to the albedo and the shading normal, driven by the plan's strength (LOOK-009) and the material weights (LOOK-008): four noise octaves with cells of 16 m, 2 m, 25 cm and 3 cm, an octave fading out while a pixel covers 0.06 to 0.25 of its cell. The octaves SHALL be anchored to the world (the node record's integer cell at 1/32 m shifted right by the octave's bits, 9, 6, 3 and 0, and the fraction; no planet-radius float in the shader, CON-11). The noise SHALL be planar value noise with analytic gradients on the plane of the lattice the surface normal lies closest to (the planes of the axes within 0.12 of it weigh in by weights continuous in the normal), the lattice of every other octave turned by 45 degrees by an integer matrix. Its cost SHALL be at most +1.0 ms of GPU time over the detail off at 2560x1440 at the eye heights 1.8 m and 0.5 m. With the strength 0 the picture SHALL be what it was before the term, bit for bit; the rest as before.
