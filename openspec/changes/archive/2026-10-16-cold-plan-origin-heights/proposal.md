## Why

Perf window 1 (2026-10-16, RTX, `perf stream all` over the 16384 store): the dive's worst plan took 9,384 ms (a cold plan at altitude) and the dive descent lag was 10.6 levels on average (budget 1) and 16.2 at worst (budget 3). The plan worker is too slow at altitude, so the drawn tiles lag the target.

The ablation (docs/learnings.md, 2026-10-16, `examples/plan_bands.rs`, release, the real eroded field through `StoreSource` over the real store, a cold source and cache per measurement) shows where a cold plan at altitude goes: not into the selection (0-1 ms above 500 km, no lattice reads: the height bounds of a coarse cap are the global range) but into the origin heights of its nodes. At 20,000 km 52 nodes need 188 distinct blocks (3.8 s at about 20 ms per block, one thread), at 5,000 km 265 nodes need 345 blocks (6.9 s), at 2,000 km 467 nodes need 155 blocks (3.1 s). Below 20 km the selection is 55-95 ms and the origins 20-45 ms. On one thread each block was already decoded once (the node footprints of a cold plan hardly overlap, so there was no thrash to remove there), but the 20 plan threads (`prefill_origin_heights`) cut the node list into 20 chunks and every thread evaluated `height` node by node against the shared 31-block cache: 352 decodes for 188 distinct blocks at 20,000 km, 536 for 345 at 5,000 km.

## What Changes

- generators: `HeightField::heights_threaded_until(dirs, stop, threads)` / `heights_threaded(dirs, threads)` (default: contiguous chunks over scoped threads, each direction evaluated with `height`); `ErosionField` and `TerrainField` override it with `eval_gathered_threads`: the footprint of the whole batch is gathered, the distinct blocks are dealt to the threads (each thread reads the cells of one block at a time, so a block is decoded once however small the cache and `threads` blocks are in flight), and the evaluation from the cell table is split over the threads in contiguous chunks. Every value is bit for bit `height` of its direction (GEN-020), for every thread count. A batch of two directions or more is gathered when a plan asks (a cold plan at altitude has tens of nodes), where the tile builders' batch keeps its 64-direction floor. `Bound` and the app's `CancelGuarded` delegate it.
- frame: the plan builder evaluates the origin heights of the nodes its `SelectionCache` lacks as ONE batch (`origin_heights`), after the selection (the selection's decisions use the tiles' height-bounds volumes, never origin heights, so no level-by-level batching is needed: one batch per plan, which is at most the tree depth), instead of one `height` per node during the plan assembly (one thread) or per chunk (the threaded prefill). Memoisation and invalidation as before (LOD-009: a field that reads cells reuses nothing; a non-finite height is never kept); a plan with nothing new makes no batch.
- cache: `StoreSource` drops the eviction epoch from its per-thread pin: a pin is valid while the thread keeps reading the same block. With many threads decoding at once the epoch moves with every insert, so a pin was stale at once, the next read of the same block went back to the shared cache and found its slot evicted (a re-decode: with the batch in place and the epoch kept, 261 decodes for 188 blocks at 20 threads; first tried: reading the epoch after the decode, which still fails for a cache smaller than the thread count). A batch that reads a block on one thread now decodes it once whatever the budget. Memory bound unchanged (a thread pins at most one block, until its next read of another block).
- app example `plan_bands` (measurement): the ablation per altitude band and the dive's plan column replayed the way the plan worker runs it.
- spec: LOD-009 and GEN-020 state the batch of origin heights (modified, no new id).

Not in this change: a partial decode or summary (an origin height needs about 5 cells of a 1,025 x 1,025 block; the 9 ms read and 12 ms decode per block remain the floor, ADR needed), a larger block cache, pre-filling coarse levels (the owner's decision), tile generation, the residency set, keys, bake format, `MESH_VERSION`, goldens, pinned counts.
