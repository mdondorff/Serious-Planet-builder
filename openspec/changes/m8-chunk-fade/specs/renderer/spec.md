## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: REND-039 A landed chunk's change fades in the draw
A tile with chunk detail SHALL carry the chunk's change per vertex beside its heights, and the vertex shader SHALL place each vertex, and the parent-grid target it morphs to, at `h_q - (1 - w) x change` along the base normal, `1 - w` the fade in the node record (the fourth lane of grid phase 6, an f32, CON-11; 0 at `w` 1, so the record does not grow and a settled node's record is what it was), the plane read from the frame's tile-reference buffer where the node's reference points (the renderer writes the plane there only for a node whose change is still fading in, at most `FADE_PLANES` (512) a frame, the rest drawing their final height and counted in `TerrainStats::fade_overflow`; one storage binding fewer than a second pool would need, so the vertex stage still reads four). With `w = 1` or without a change plane the arithmetic SHALL be the old one bit for bit, so a world without chunks draws as it did, and at `w = 0` the drawn height SHALL be the base's. The normals stay those of the final surface (design decision 4). The CPU mirror (`CompactTile::shade_vertex_faded`) SHALL form the same f32 operations, and the reference renderer is checked by drawing a product mesh of the blended field (its plan's weight is not read). A change plane SHALL hold one value per texel (a tile that does not is refused), a plane with a value that is not finite SHALL be refused naming the texel, and the CPU mirror SHALL form no `fade x change` at fade 0; the planes of a frame SHALL be given stickily (`FadePlaneGrants`): a tile that holds a plane keeps it while it fades, a tile left without one stays without until its fade ends, and a new one is granted in tile-id order while there is room, so a node past the limit is never switched between its faded and its final height mid-fade. Known limit: two tiles at different weights, or a tile next to a chunk that is not ready (weight 0 while its neighbour is at its weight), differ along their shared edge by up to the change times the weight difference, which lasts as long as the neighbour chunk takes; the skirt (0.08 of the tile edge, about 12 m at level 16 and 49 m at level 14) covers only the steps of the finer levels, not the 58 m of the largest valley, until `m8-chunk-fade-increment`.

Verify: A, C
Status: active
Source: M8 exit criterion 3, ADR 0077 point 7, STRM-039 (the weight), GEN-025, REND-031

#### Scenario: The fade in the draw
- **WHEN** a tile with a change plane is drawn at `w` 0, 0.5 and 1 on the software adapter, and a tile without one is drawn
- **THEN** the drawn heights are the base's, the base plus half the change, and the final heights (the compact renderer's image equals the reference renderer's image of a product mesh of the blended field, which uses its own arithmetic: no pixel differs by more than the test's tolerance of 0.4 %, 0 measured); the tile without a plane is bit-identical to the old draw, and so is a tile with one at weight 1; the mirror's vertices are the final minus fade times change; and the step per frame of a fade over `chunk_fade_ms` at 60 frames per second is at most the weight step times the largest change (0.19 m for the 256 bake's largest entry step of 17.55 m, 0.65 m for the shared store's 58.3 m, which is 0.20 px on screen where it enters: `design.md` 6a; a measurement of the scene, not an assertion of the test)
