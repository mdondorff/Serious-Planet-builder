## Decisions
- The cap is the streamer's capacity, not a second mechanism. The streamer already evicts in the order the order asks for (STRM-007: tiles outside D, R and the prefetch set, least recently used first, then R tiles farthest and finest first; D with its ancestors and the roots never). It evicts only to make room, and the viewer's capacity was the renderer's pool (205,170 slots on the mid tier). Lowering the capacity makes the existing rule fire; no new eviction code, no new order, no new state.
- Cap value 24,000 tiles. R with its ancestors for the ten standard views is at most 10 % of the mid tier, 20,517 slots (STRM-006 headroom), and the ground-1m draw set at most 6 %, 12,310. 24,000 leaves about 3,500 slots (17 %) of the largest R for the prefetch set, jobs in flight and the tiles retiring over the frames in flight, so a settled view is the exact plan and eviction changes no pixel. The high tier's pool (314,594 slots) is far above it and gets the same cap. The cap sits below the 15,000 to 28,000 range where crusader's stalls were 2.4 to 10 s only if the render-submit time follows the resident count: that is what G-RT measures, and the value is restated from the measurement if the stalls do not go.
- Where it applies: the window's rig only (`Rig::new_with_progress` takes the cap; `Rig::new` for the perf scenes does not). The perf scenes keep the pool as their capacity so their numbers stay comparable with the logs of record.
- `--resident-cap N` overrides it, at most the pool: `resident_capacity = min(pool, N)`. A cap below the roots (384 at floor 3) or below D with its ancestors is a budget failure the streamer reports already (STRM-007, "Budget failure"); no new check.

## Tests
- Tier A, frame crate: the managed loop on fakes walks 60 stops with a capacity of twice the widest view plus 10 and with a pool 100 times that: the capped run stays inside the cap in every frame, shows no blank frame, evicts, and settles at the exact plan; the pool run grows past the cap and evicts nothing.
- Tier A, app crate: `resident_capacity` (pool, cap and a small pool) and the cap against R of the standard views.
- Not asserted: the stall count. G-RT replays `frame-17426-path.txt` over `target/stores/r3a-e1` in the window (BUILD-054), once with `--resident-cap 1000000` (the pool) and once with the default; the numbers go to the report.
