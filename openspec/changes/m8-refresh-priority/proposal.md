## Why
In the orbit to 1 m flight on the shared store (four runs, 2026-10-26, RTX, window, `m8-chunk-fade-publish` evidence) a landed chunk's change is drawn 10 to 80 s after it landed, with 133 to 352 tiles waiting. The change is drawn only when the tiles that read the chunk have been rebuilt (STRM-052: the clock of the fade starts then), so the rebuild after a landing is the limiter of how fast chunk detail appears; criteria 3 and 7 count drawn detail. The cause assumed from the code is the queue: `Streamer::refresh` puts a resident tile back in the queue under its class, and a refresh of a drawn tile (class Draw, rank 0) waits beside the missing tiles of the descent, which come in hundreds a second and share the workers with the chunk jobs. This is not measured yet.

## What Changes
1. **Measure first.** Per refresh: the time in the queue (request to dispatch) and in the job (dispatch to finish), summed per frame in the stats and the log (`StreamStats::refresh_wait_ms`, `refresh_run_ms`, counts), so that the flight says whether the wait or the work is long. Same flight script, 4 minutes, as before.
2. **The lever, if the wait is long:** a refresh of a tile that is drawn and stale takes the front of the Draw class ahead of a missing tile whose coarser ancestor is drawn (the missing tile adds detail the picture has already, the stale one holds back the chunk's change). The order stays a pure function of the wants (CON-14). If the work is long (a tile that reads nine chunk pyramids), the lever is another one (the increment on the CPU without a rebuild, or caching the chunk reads per tile) and this change says so and stops.
3. **Before and after** by the same flight script: the longest and median landing to drawn (`PublishStats` of the closed branch, restored as a log line), the number of tiles waiting at the peak, and the stand-in share of the descent (`FrameCounts::chunk`), so the priority does not starve the new tiles of the descent.

## Impact
- Spec: STRM-0xx (refresh order), tier A on the fake clock for the order; the flight numbers are a recorded measurement, not an assertion.
- Code: `streaming/src/lib.rs` (the queue key of a refresh; counters), `frame/managed.rs` (which refreshes are of drawn tiles), `app/window.rs` (the log line).
- Cost: about one day for the measurement and the order; the flight is run by the crusader on the owner's machine while the owner is away.
