## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: TEST-056 The edit-latency scene runs with the chunk stage
The `edit-latency` scene (TEST-019) SHALL, given a store and unless `--no-chunks` is given, run its world with the viewer's chunk stage (the pool, the chunk configuration and the horizon jobs the window uses, `anetworld::viewer_chunk_stage`) and drive the chunk frames in its loop, with the hold of the stale chunks while the edit's tiles are built unless `--no-stale-hold` is given. In such a run the budget table's drawn-exact figure SHALL be the instant the last of the tiles the edit names that the plan draws (before the edit, or at the first plan delivered after it) ended its first refresh job in the streamer's job log, plus the median frame time of the run for the frame that uploads it (ADR 0072 point 1; an estimate of the upload, not an observation of it), not the instant the plan is exact (which waits for the stale chunks to land); when no drawn tile was rebuilt it SHALL be the instant every tile the edit names was refreshed and written. That instant, the instant every named tile was refreshed and written and the instant nothing is left to do stay columns of the stages table, recorded and not gated. The scene SHALL also print, for the first run of each case, the refresh frame by frame (the instant, the frame time, the tiles refreshed, the jobs in flight and the uploads waiting, for the frames in which a count moved or the frame took over 30 ms) and the last ten refresh jobs that ended within 2.5 s of the edit by tile, with their waits and run times. After the budget and stages tables it SHALL print a table of the chunk side of every case: the longest chunk frame after the edit, the chunks that were solved again, the instants of the chunk under the edit centre and of the last chunk, and the instant the tiles that read them were drawn again, with a verdict against 10 s. The instant the tiles that read them were drawn again SHALL be the later of the last chunk landing and the end of the last refresh job of a tile the plan draws at the end of the case (ADR 0072 point 1: the tiles the plan does not draw refresh in the background), plus the median frame time for the upload; the table SHALL give beside it the instant nothing was left to do (every resident tile refreshed, nothing in flight), recorded and not gated.

Verify: A
Status: active
Source: m8-edit-latency-chunks, M8 criterion 6, TEST-019, TEST-041

#### Scenario: The chunk table
- **WHEN** the table is made from cases whose runs carry chunk readings
- **THEN** it gives the medians per case, `ok` where the eroded result is drawn within 10 s and `over` where it is not, and `-` for a case without a chunk stage
