## Why
M8 exit criterion 7 asks for the speed at which detail arrives late. The envelope scene (TEST-047) measures it for tiles only: the app builds no chunk manager for the scene's world (`chunk_view_of` is `None`, the log says `chunk_field: false`), so the chunk stand-in share (STRM-039) is never counted and the curve of #619 (70, 250, 540, 1600 m/s at 30 m, 100 m, 300 m, 1 km) is the tile curve. In the viewer a flight also waits for chunks (about 3.3 s at 8 m on 4 threads, 5.3 s on one, GEN-052): the chunk curve is what M8-8's window must record.

## What Changes
Proposal for the planner's review; no code before the answer.
1. **A chunk stage in the scene's world.** After `open_world` (a `--store` bake with a stored coupled run, as `anet-chunk` and `capture stills`), the scene calls the stage's setup that `capture stills` has in `stills::enable_chunks` (coupled run ready, `enable_anet`, no horizon unless asked): that function moves to a shared module (`chunkstage.rs`) and both callers use it. Without a store the scene keeps today's tile-only behaviour and says so in the log (`chunk_field: false`).
2. **The frame drives the chunk jobs.** `Driver::tick` (streamperf) gets what `window.rs` does after `begin_frame`: the plan's tiles go to `world.chunk_frame(&tiles, camera.position, &[])`, `managed.chunk_landed(&update.landed)` and `chunk_evicted`. The scene sets `set_chunk_view(world.chunk_view())` as today. Prefetch (STRM-042) uses what the viewer passes (nothing yet), so the curve is the viewer's.
3. **The settle.** The start view waits, as now, until the tiles are quiet; with a chunk stage it also waits for the chunks of the first plan (the same rule as a still: every tile of the plan reads only ready chunks), so a trial starts from a settled view and measures what arrives during the flight.
4. **Cold ground per trial.** The tile cache is empty at a trial's start (as now) and so is the chunk cache for the new ground: each trial has its own start place 67 km apart (`PLACE_STEP_DEG`), the chunk manager's byte cap evicts old chunks; the manager is rebuilt (or its cache cleared) at each trial so no chunk of an earlier flight is resident.
5. **The judged share.** The bound stays on the larger of the pixel-weighted coarse share and the node-weighted chunk stand-in fraction (already in `Measure::judged`). The log gains per trial: chunks landed, the median and the largest chunk job time, the chunk jobs still in flight at the end.
6. **Worker split.** Chunk jobs run on their own pool (at most half the workers, STRM-046 as in the viewer), so tile builds and chunk solves compete as they do in the window. The horizon jobs (REND-038) are not run by default; `--horizon` adds them (their cost is about 5 s on 6 threads per chunk, which would dominate the curve): the planner decides whether M8-8's window uses it.

## Impact
- Spec: testing, one new requirement TEST-054 (the envelope scene runs the chunk stage when the world has a stored coupled run); TEST-047 keeps its text.
- Code: `app/src/envelopeperf.rs` (setup, per-trial reset, log fields), `app/src/streamperf.rs` (`Driver::tick` chunk step), `app/src/stills.rs` (`enable_chunks` moves to `chunkstage.rs`).
- Tests: tier A on fakes (the per-frame chunk step calls `chunk_frame` once, passes landed and evicted on; the log fields), tier B smoke on an in-memory bake (`--allow-memory-bake --global-res`, coarse chunks) that the scene's log says `chunk_field: true` and counts chunk nodes. Never a measurement by an agent.
- Cost of M8-8's window, estimated: 4 altitudes x (1 + 4 bisection) trials, each a settle of the start view's chunks (about 10-20 s on 4 workers) plus a 12 s flight: about 25-35 min; the architect authorizes it.
- Open questions for the planner: (a) horizon jobs in the window (default off); (b) one chunk cache for the whole run versus cleared per trial (proposed: cleared, so no trial benefits from the last); (c) the chunk cell size: 8 m (the viewer's) unless told.
