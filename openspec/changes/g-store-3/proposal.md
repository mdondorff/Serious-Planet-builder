## Why

## What Changes
- `generators/examples/edit_cases`: `AUX`, the key table, border summaries, edit overrides and continuity map of A3 section 10 as counts and bytes at stated layouts.
- `cache/examples/store_space`: `sphere` mode, the field bake over every stride-th tile of all six faces (the land share taken from the samples), ending after the field bake.
