## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: GEN-175 Per-world epsilon of the f32 rounds
The f32 rounds (GEN-166) SHALL store the heights as they are, in metres above the sea level of the carve's frame (0), so the f32 spacing is finest at the coasts, outlets, lakes and the K7 floor. The flood epsilon of the world SHALL be `epsilon_w = max(epsilon_profile, k x s(R) x (1 + m))` with `s(R)` the gap from `R` to the next f32 above it (infinite when `R` does not fit an f32), `R = max |h|` over the world's height bounds, `k = 1` and `m` a relative margin of 2^-20 above the f64 rounding of a height, fixed from the world's definition (never per window or tile, CON-30). The bounds SHALL hold every height a round can produce (the base heights plus the profile's largest uplift). The rounds SHALL run in f32 unless `epsilon_w` exceeds the named parameter `epsilon_max` (or does not exist), and then in f64 with `epsilon_profile`; a span whose `epsilon_w` is at most `epsilon_max` SHALL stay f32. `epsilon_max` SHALL be at least `s(R)` of a 60 km span above the sea. The choice SHALL be part of the bake key through the epsilon and the f32 flag of the carve parameters, and `R` and `epsilon_w` part of the run's MARK line. With `epsilon_w = epsilon_profile` every result and key SHALL be the bits of the build before this requirement (Earth's span).
A plan made for known bounds SHALL flood with `epsilon_w` in the carve, H1's fill and the sediment pass (K7 through `apply_k7`), and SHALL refuse a landmass or a surface after the rounds with a height over `R`, naming the cell. Every f32 narrowing SHALL refuse a surface whose f32 spacing at its largest height is not under the epsilon of the round, naming the cell. A filled surface SHALL never be narrowed or stored as f32 (the surface gate refuses it), and a plan SHALL be made for the bounds of its world.
The carve is sensitive to the flood epsilon and to sub-millimetre perturbations of its start surface at large relief (cascades of breach and flat routes from the third round on). A difference between two runs at that scale is the carve's property, not a defect of the rounding: f32 against f64 at the same epsilon is judged after one round (at most 2 x `s(R)`) and, after several, against the noise floor of f64 under a second jitter seed (the start surface perturbed by 0.2 mm of noise). The measurements are in the learning of 2026-11-12.
