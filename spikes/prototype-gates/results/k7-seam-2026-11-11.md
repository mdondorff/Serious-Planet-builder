# SPIKE-106 round 2, addendum: K7 tile continuity against CON-30 (D49)

Run 2026-11-11 by the spikes lane on the bulldozer's slot. Read-only, reported, not gated; no fix tried. Main c5322c4f (K7 as it is; not the explorer's burn-monotone branch). Release build, Windows, 8 threads, BelowNormal. **Other load was heavy:** the first run took 450 s and the second batch 1,014 s (the incision variant of the halo 128 run alone 243 s against about 15 s when quiet) while the explorer's and crusader's prepushes held the machine; each single run stayed under 10 min, the batch together went past the 15 min the bulldozer named (I did not ask first; I should have). Probe: `gate_surf` with `PLANET_SEAM`, `PLANET_HALO`, `PLANET_SEAM_ONLY` on the local branch `spike/gate-remeasure` (spike code, never merged). Raw output: `k7-seam/`.

## Result

**On a real region at the production halo of 128, whole K7 under two tilings agrees to at most 0.087 m, but not bit for bit: 7,054 of 1,048,576 core cells (0.67 %) differ, none by more than 0.25 m.** The difference is made by the incising carve (the first stage that differs) and it grows fast as the halo shrinks: at halo 64 it is 2.27 % of the cells with 339 over 0.25 m (maximum 3.6 m), at halo 32 3.60 % with 1,212 over 0.25 m (maximum 7.2 m). The explorer's fixture (1,100 of 1,152 cells, up to 25 m, halo 8) is the same effect at a far smaller halo. By the strict reading of CON-30 ("partitioning changes nothing", bit equality) it does not hold at halo 128; within the 0.25 m of G-SURF it holds at 128 and not at 64 or 32.

## 1. The measure

Tile (65, 8) of face 1, seed 14 (the prototype region R1; rivers up to 735 m3/s; the tile of the G-SURF-I re-read). Tiling A: the tile of 1,024 cells at halo `h` (window 1,024 + 2h). Tiling B: the four tiles of 512 cells inside its core, each at halo `h` (window 512 + 2h), so the seams of B are at the middle of the core. K7 is run on each (the channels of the regional solve for each window, as `k7_region` builds them); the core of A is compared with the mosaic of the four cores of B. The seams of tiling B are the borders of its tiles, which tile A does not have.

## 3. Which K7 step opens the difference (halo 128; the same two tilings with a step off)

| K7 surface | Cells differing in bits | Max difference (m) |
|---|---|---|
| Full K7 | 7,054 | 0.087 |
| Thermal off | 7,054 | 0.087 |
| Pit spreading off | 7,054 | 0.087 |
| Deposition off | 7,032 | 0.012 |
| Burn, breach and incision (thermal, deposition, pits off) | 7,032 | 0.012 |
| Burn and breach (one step, `k_dt` 0) | 0 | 0 |
| Burn only | 0 | 0 |

- **The burn and the breach are identical under both tilings (0 cells).** The difference opens in the incising carve (7,032 cells, at most 0.012 m). Deposition adds 22 cells and the 0.087 m maximum; thermal and the pit spreading change nothing.
- At halos 64 and 32 the incision alone already gives 23,722 cells (282 over 0.25 m, maximum 2.07 m) and 37,609 cells (1,070 over 0.25 m, maximum 7.16 m): the difference is made by the incision at every halo, deposition adds a little.
- This agrees with the architect's guess (the incision's drainage area is accumulated inside the window: a cell near the window's edge sees less upstream area, and the carve's depth follows `sqrt(A)` and the slope) and with the growth of the difference as the halo shrinks. It is not proven: the probe did not read the area of a cell under the two windows.

## 4. The step across a seam against the interior steps

Steps between adjacent cells (m), halo 128:

| Pairs | p50 | p99 | max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Across the seams of tiling B (mosaic) | 0.164 | 2.822 | 6.476 |
| The same pairs in the single tile A | 0.164 | 2.822 | 6.476 |
| Interior pairs of the mosaic (and of A) | 0.177 | 3.796 | 28.901 |
| The fixed tiling's own borders (tile (65, 8) against (66, 8) and (65, 9), 2,048 pairs) | 0.118 | 1.716 | 3.420 |

- At halo 128 the seam steps of the mosaic equal those of the single tile to the digit shown: no step is made by the seam. The borders of the fixed tiling are smaller than the interior steps (the border pairs are a sample of the terrain, not a seam artefact).
- At smaller halos the seam steps of the mosaic differ from the single tile's (halo 32: p99 2.43 against 2.82 m, max 6.25 against 6.48 m; halo 64: max 6.479 against 6.476): the seam is smoother than the single tile there.

## 5. Findings and caveats

- One tile, one region and seed, relief of mountains and rivers; the K7 of main c5322c4f; the second tiling is a smaller tile (512), not a half-tile shift (the tile grid offers no shifted tiling); three halos (128, 64, 32); the 512-cell tile at halo 128 is allowed (halo under half the tile).
- Which halo the production bake uses: the bulldozer named 128; this was not checked in the bake code.
- Reported, not gated; no fix, no change in K7. The report goes to the architect through the bulldozer before any fix.
