Source: architecture report M4 row ("Dive test: no blank tiles, p99 frame <= 20 ms, VRAM within tier budget, RAM <= 8 GB") and risk table ("orbit to ground in < 30 s", "p99 tile latency <= 200 ms"); owner's remarks 2026-10-13; SPIKE-05 (`docs/spikes.md`); briefing "Known facts for M4" (`docs/briefings/sonnet-explorer-m4.md`); `docs/learnings.md` 2026-10-15 (GPU clock); `docs/background/optimization-brainstorm-review.md` item A; STRM-002, TEST-011, TEST-012, TEST-013; constitution CON-04, CON-17, CON-20, CON-21, CON-22; ADRs 0020, 0031, 0033

## Context
M4's exit test names a dive but not its profile. A constant speed is meaningless across seven decades of altitude. The owner also named two cases that velocity prefetch cannot help: a teleport ("portal") and erratic mouse look. "No blank tiles" alone does not catch a view that stays blurry for seconds, so each case also gets a detail-lag budget.

Measured facts this ADR is built on (counts and ad hoc timings; nothing here is a perf-window verdict unless it says so):
- **Plan cost.** A cold plan at ground-1m (fresh per-tile cache) took 28.6 ms (2026-10-11) and 25.6 ms (2026-10-12) in perf windows against 6-7 ms warm (reference field; budget 8 ms warm). With the eroded field over the 16384 store (SPIKE-05, ad hoc): draw plan cold 184 ms and warm 20 ms at ground-1m, cold 59-121 ms at aerial-20km to ground-200m, and 0.7 to 6.3 s warm (6.5 s cold at orbit) at aerial-200km to orbit. Queue item 7a (compute origin height and mesh key once per tile) is meant to fix the warm figures; it does not make a first plan of an unseen place free.
- **Fill time.** From an empty cache the ground-1m draw set (8,763 tiles) takes about 7.2 s on 20 threads with a lock-free source (1,217 eroded tiles/s), about 400 s through `StoreSource` as it is (22 tiles/s). The 2 km draw set (2,625 tiles) takes about 2.2 s at the lock-free rate. Both counts are leaves of level 26; requests include the ancestors (up to one third more tiles, ADR 0033) and ADR 0033 point 6 caps the finest level at 22, so this ADR fixes the fixture at **cells 32, finest level 22** and counts ancestors: ground-1m 6,633 leaves, about 8,850 with ancestors, 7.3 s at the lock-free rate; 2 km 2,625, about 3,500, 2.9 s. Item 7b (store-source concurrency) is the prerequisite for any fill-time budget below.
- **Upload.** ADR 0033 point 9: 1 MiB per frame (about 118 slots of 8.8 KB), 2 ms criterion (CON-21); a full ground-1m fill is 77 MB, 74 frames at the cap, short against the 7.2 s generation fill. Generation, not upload, limits a teleport.
- **Residency.** ADR 0031: the residency set R depends on the position only; rotation never fetches (one exception: its point 4c, a share too small for the draw set, which these cases do not exercise); requests are ordered missing draw-set nodes (ancestors first), rest of R, prefetch.
- **GPU clock.** GPU milliseconds move with the clock state (ground-1m 2.9-3.0 ms at P0 about 1.4 GHz, 4.1-4.2 ms at P2/P3 about 1.15 GHz; the same code read 2.85-6.0 ms across runs in SPIKE-06). Queue item 6b makes the harness record clock and P-state per view and compare GPU medians only at equal clock state.

## Decision (proposed)
1. **Dive.** Altitude above the terrain falls by a constant factor per unit time (equal time per decade): from 20,000 km to 1 m in 30 s (7.3 decades, 4.1 s per decade; the altitude-time function is the shape of TEST-013's log-spaced dive, the path above the terrain is one function `dive_camera(t)` shared by tests, the perf scene and the flight review), then a **ground segment of 10 s at 1 m altitude at 1.5 m/s** (walking). A second ground variant at **30 m/s** (vehicle, `dive-fast`) is a stress case that is run and reported with its numbers but has **no detail-lag budget**: at 30 m/s the finest ring turns over in about 0.1 s, which by estimate needs far more than 1,217 tiles/s, and the budget is set from the first measurement (a declared addition to this ADR). Fixed frame step 1/60 s on the fake clock; the camera looks along the direction of travel with a fixed pitch (no look input). 40 s in all.
2. **Teleport.** Start at rest at 2 km altitude over a position whose tiles are resident and the fill complete (settled, 10 s). At t = 0 the camera jumps to the antipodal position at (a) **1 m** and (b) **2 km** altitude (two cases; the destination is cold in RAM, the persistent cache has nothing), and holds still for 10 s (600 frames). The first frame after the jump is part of the case. *Settled* means R fully resident, no request queued or in flight, for one second on the fake clock.
3. **Mouse look.** At a fixed position (1 m and 2 km altitude), after R is complete (settled), a scripted 10 s sequence (a self-contained integer PRNG written for the test, not `libm` or a platform generator, so identical on every run and platform) of yaw and pitch steps with reversals, ending with a 180 degree yaw within 0.2 s. A second run starts the same sequence **during** the fill from empty (no settle) to check that rotation does not change what is fetched.
4. **Measures, per case, all per frame unless stated:**

| measure | budget | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Blank tiles** | none, ever: every visible node has a resident tile of its own or of an ancestor (STRM-002); and no clear-colour pixel below the horizon (tier B) | hard, every tier |
| **Frame time** | p99 <= 20 ms; no frame over 40 ms (a threshold introduced by this ADR, two frame budgets, not in CON-21 or the exit criteria); median <= 16.6 ms (CON-21), over the whole case including the first frame after a jump | perf window (ADR 0020) |
| **Upload** | bytes per frame <= the cap (1 MiB, ADR 0033 point 9); upload time <= 2 ms | bytes: tier A; time: perf window |
| **Memory** | allocated terrain bytes <= the tier's share (ADR 0033), every frame; renderer-allocated VRAM <= the tier cap (8 GB / 14 GB); driver-reported VRAM <= the cap; process working set <= 8 GB (CON-21) | share and renderer allocation: tier A (counted); driver VRAM and working set: perf window |
| **Detail lag** | below | tier A/B on fakes, perf window on hardware |

   **Detail lag** is, per frame, the area-weighted mean over the visible screen of (target level minus drawn level), where target is the level the plan selects with every tile resident and drawn is the level of the resident tile actually used (an ancestor if the node is missing); the area is the node's projected pixels in the frame. Per case, with the generation rate of the model at 1,217 tiles/s (the measured lock-free rate) and the cache cold where stated:

| case | budget (detail lag) |
|---|---|
| dive, descent | mean over the descent <= 1 level; no frame over 3 levels |
| dive, ground segment at 1.5 m/s | back to 0 (draw set complete) within 2 s after the end of the descent, then 0 for the rest of the segment; derived by estimate only (new-tile demand of a walking camera at the finest ring is of the order of 400-800 tiles/s, below the model rate), so the first model run confirms or re-proposes it |
| dive, ground segment at 30 m/s | reported only, no budget |
| teleport to 2 km | frame 0: any lag, but a tile for every visible node; under 1 level within 1 s; draw set with its ancestors complete (lag 0) within 4 s |
| teleport to 1 m | frame 0: any lag, but a tile for every visible node; under 1 level within 2 s; draw set with its ancestors complete within 9 s (7.3 s of generation plus margin) |
| mouse look, settled | 0 in every frame; zero requests, zero cancellations, zero uploads (rotation never fetches, ADR 0031) |
| mouse look, from empty | once the fill completes, the set of tile ids requested over the whole run equals the still camera's (rotation only reorders, never adds or drops), and each request's class equals the still camera's; no lag budget (the lag after a turn toward a region not yet filled is legitimately higher) |

   These budgets are proposals fixed from the measured rates; the first fake-clock run and the first perf window may show that one does not hold. The implementation change then reports the numbers and re-proposes the budget here as a declared change, and never loosens it to get green.
5. **Teleport and the frame budget: not exempt; the cold plan leaves the frame's critical path.** Measurement: a cold plan at ground-1m is 25.6-28.6 ms with the reference field (perf windows) and 184 ms with the eroded field (SPIKE-05, ad hoc), up to seconds at altitude, against warm 6-7 ms (reference) and 20 ms (eroded) and a 20 ms p99. Two facts decide it. (a) With the reference-field cost a single cold frame is 1.5-1.7 frame times (at 16.6 ms) and passes both p99 (a 600-frame hold allows six frames over 20 ms) and the 40 ms cap, so an exemption would not even be needed. (b) With the eroded field the same frame is 9 frames at 184 ms, a visible freeze that an exemption would hide, and the plan time of an unseen place cannot be bounded by caching (the first plan has to evaluate each tile once). The rule therefore is: **no frame waits for a plan** (frame pipelining, queue item 7; STRM-002's "never block a frame"): the frame after a jump draws a **cover plan**: the selection restricted to resident tiles (each visible node by its deepest resident ancestor), which evaluates no field and is bounded by the resident count, produced by the plan worker before the full plan; the full plan arrives some frames later. This is ADR 0031 point 5's stale-R frame made concrete. At 1 m the camera lies below the coarse surface of a root tile (LOD-008), so frame 0 may show that surface from below: the pixel check of tier B excludes frame 0 of the 1 m teleport and reports what it shows; the tile check (STRM-002) holds in every frame. How the cover handles the camera below a coarse surface is open for the implementation. The perf scene reports the teleport case's plan time apart from streaming and upload time, as a column per frame (plan, scheduler, upload, record, GPU, wall), so a frame over budget is attributed to its largest part. Until items 7a, 7b and the pipelined loop are merged the teleport perf scene is not run for a verdict; a cold plan on the frame path is reported as OVER and is not excused.
6. **Plan, stream and upload are separate columns** in every case. The dive's per-frame plans are nearly warm once running (altitude shrinks by under 1 % per frame, the per-tile cache keeps its entries), but the **first** plan at 20,000 km is cold (6.5 s with the eroded field today, SPIKE-05) and the warm plans at aerial-200km to orbit are 0.7-6.3 s until item 7a: the dive's p99 and 40 ms cap therefore also wait for 7a and the pipelined loop. A teleport is the case with a fully cold plan in mid-run, which is why it proves the rule of point 5.
7. **GPU clock (queue item 6b).** The perf scenes record the clock and P-state before and after each case (the harness does). A case measured outside P0 is marked not valid for GPU budgets (wall-clock p99 still reported), and the regression report compares GPU medians only between sessions at equal clock state. Detail-lag budgets are about counts and the fake or measured generation rate, never about GPU time, so they are unaffected.
8. **Tiers** (CON-20: counts everywhere, time only on the reference machine):
   - **A (any machine, fast and full):** all cases on the fake clock, fake I/O with 200 ms latency (STRM-002's scenario), a fake spawner with a generation-rate model (1,217 tiles/s, plus a 22 tiles/s run that documents today's `StoreSource` and is expected to exceed the lag budgets, reported not asserted) and a cheap fixture (the reference field or a 1024-cell in-memory bake), no GPU. Asserts blank tiles, requests, cancellations, upload bytes per frame, allocated slots against the share, detail lag, the order of the fill. Plan time is not asserted here.
   - **B (software adapter):** the same scripts rendered at 160x120, every tenth frame (to fit the tier-B time budget; the tile check of tier A covers every frame): no clear-colour pixel below the horizon in any frame, counts the renderer reports equal the plan's (TEST-011).
   - **C and perf (Rasierklinge):** `cargo xtask test gpu --real` runs the cases for blank tiles and counts only (no timing). The perf scenes `dive`, `dive-fast`, `teleport-1m`, `teleport-2km`, `look-1m`, `look-2km` run in `cargo xtask perf` (the architect's window) with the real clock, real threads and the default eroded world (queue item 7d), and report the measures of point 4 as wall time, with the thread count and the clock state in the log.
9. Count-based measures never depend on the machine; every time-based verdict is a perf-window verdict and is invalid with a failed gate or throttling (ADR 0020).

## Owner decision needed
M4's exit criteria stay as the architecture report states them: "Dive test: no blank tiles, p99 frame <= 20 ms, VRAM within tier budget, RAM <= 8 GB". The dive of point 1 is that test (with no detail-lag gate: that is part of the proposal below). **Proposed addition (the owner decides):** the teleport (both altitudes) and the mouse-look cases of points 2 and 3 are added to the exit criteria with the same four hard measures (no blank tiles, p99 frame <= 20 ms, VRAM within the share and tier, RAM <= 8 GB), and the detail-lag budgets of point 4 (a new kind of criterion for the dive as well) are reported in the M4 report and gate the milestone for the dive's descent and the mouse look but are tracked for the teleport, because the teleport's fill time depends on the machine's thread count. Alternatives: (1) keep the exit criteria as they are, the dive gating on its four measures only and every other case and budget tracked; (2) gate every budget of point 4 including the teleport's fill times. Recommended: the proposal above. Whichever is chosen, the cases and measures are built and reported.

## Consequences
- New perf scenes as point 8; tier-A twins with fake I/O at 200 ms latency. Building them is part of queue item 7 (after the scheduler, residency manager, pipelined loop and upload budget exist).
- The perf verdicts of the teleport and of the dive wait for items 7a, 7b and frame pipelining; the other budgets are asserted at tier A once the scheduler exists. Before 7a and 7b, SPIKE-05's numbers say the 2 s detail-lag budget at 1 m is unattainable (400 s fill as it is), and this ADR claims none.
- If the owner adds the cases to the exit criteria the M4 report shows them; otherwise they stay tracked measures in the report.
- Open for the implementation: whether the 1440p internal resolution (CON-21) or the 1080p LOD parameters feed the detail-lag areas (use the perf scene's, 2560x1440, tau 6); the exact seeded generator of the mouse script; a cache-warm teleport variant (destination tiles in the persistent pack, so generation is a read) is not defined here and would be a later case.
