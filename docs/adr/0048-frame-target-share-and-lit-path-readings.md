# 0048 Frame-target share (amends ADR 0033's remainder) and the lit path's readings of ADR 0038 and ADR 0042
Status: proposed (sonnet-explorer, 2026-10-16, M5 item 2 `render-graph-hdr`; reversible: a constant, a formula and the unit table; build on it, accept with the next planner pass)
Date: 2026-10-16
Source: ADR 0033 (terrain share 2 / 3 GiB, "later shares come from that remainder, a later ADR"); ADR 0038 points 1-4; ADR 0042 points 1-3 and 5; ADR 0035; ADR 0037; constitution CON-03, CON-04, CON-11, CON-12, CON-14, CON-20

## Context
ADR 0042 point 5 names the frame targets (HDR 29.5 MB, depth 14.7 MB, LUTs, the aerial-perspective volume, the histogram) a count-based share "asserted at tier A from the compiled graph, recorded as an amendment to ADR 0033's reserved remainder when the change lands". Writing the lit path also needed a few decisions the ADR texts leave open or read two ways.

## Decision
1. **Frame-target share** (the amendment): the bytes of every frame target the compiled frame graph allocates besides terrain's pools and the presentable target (transient resources unaliased, plus persistent ones such as the exposure history) stay at or below `40 B x pixels + 32 MiB` of the internal resolution (`render::graph::frame_target_share_bytes`): 181,010,432 B at 1440p, 2.8 % of the 6 GiB the mid tier leaves after terrain's 2 GiB. Today's lit frame uses 12 B per pixel plus 272 B (HDR `Rgba16Float` 8, depth `Depth32Float` 4, histogram 256 B, exposure history 16 B): 44,237,072 B at 1440p. The cap leaves room for the water pass's copies, the sky's targets and the atmosphere's volumes; a change that raises the count beyond it fails with the numbers (tier A, `lit_frame_graph(..).compile().frame_target_bytes()`) and the renderer's own allocation is asserted equal to the graph's count (tier C). The presentable target (the swap chain, or the renderer's `Rgba8Unorm` colour target offscreen) is not a frame target of this share: it exists on the debug path too. The LUT and volume bytes join the same graph when their passes land (items 5 and 7).
2. **Units** (ADR 0038 point 1 left them implicit): scene radiance is in units of the top-of-atmosphere solar illuminance; one unit is 126,600 lux (1361 W/m2 at 93 lm/W), so a unit of diffuse radiance is 126,600 cd/m2. Exposure follows the saturation-based convention: display-referred = luminance x `1 / (1.2 x 2^EV100)`, and the EV100 that suits a luminance L (and the histogram's axis, -10 to +20) is `log2(8 L)`. These are look data in spirit (ADR 0042 point 6) but live as constants in `frame::look` until the look file exists (item 3).
3. **The pre-exposure is decided on the CPU, in the plan.** `FramePlan` carries a `LitPlan`: the exposure mode, `dt`, the sun direction, a reset flag and the tone-map mode. For a fixed exposure the pre-exposure EV100 is the fixed EV100 (the tone map then multiplies by exactly 1). For the adaptive exposure the plan names `pre_ev100`, the EV100 the scene target is written at, chosen by the view (testkit views, perf scenes, the viewer's flight; later from the sun and the altitude), never read back from the GPU (ADR 0038 point 2: no readback). The tone map exposes `written x 2^(pre - ev)` where `ev` is the adapted EV100 the compute pass holds. f16 then holds about +-12 stops around the pre-exposure; a view that leaves that band updates `pre_ev100`.
4. **Histogram and adaptation** (ADR 0038 point 2, made exact): 64 equal bins of pixel EV100 over -10 to +20; pixels without light are not counted; out-of-range pixels land in the end bins; the mean EV of the band between 40 % and 90 % of the counted pixels (bins weighted by the share of their count inside the band, bin centres as values); target = the mean clamped to the EV100 range; the step moves the adapted EV at most `speed_up x dt` up (brighter scene) or `speed_down x dt` down, linear in EV, snapping on a reset and on the first lit frame. Fixed exposure culls the histogram pass in the graph compile (the adaptation reads no histogram), not in the executor.
5. **Lit terrain placeholder** (until item 4): Lambert of the vertex normal under the plan's sun on the relief view's height tint scaled by 0.4, no ambient, no atmosphere; the pre-exposure factor and the sun direction travel in the globals' unused words (`range.z`, `mode.yzw` as bits), so the globals buffer and every accounted size stay as they are. Item 4 replaces the albedo and the sun's source.
6. **Goldens of the lit frames** are classed `lit` in `tests/goldens/classes.txt`, so the `golden-review` tool escalates them as first goldens (ADR 0017); `tests/goldens/pending.txt` lists them until the owner's `/bless`. ADR 0037 point 1 would allow blessing them under policy, but the tool does not implement that path (it escalates every first golden and every lit view): the planner decides whether to teach the tool ADR 0037's conditions or to bless by `/bless`.

## Consequences
- The share is a test, not a promise: growth shows up in the graph tests with numbers.
- The viewer window still shows the debug path; it blits the renderer's colour target, which the lit path's tone map also writes, so showing the lit path later needs no new plumbing.
- ADR 0038 and ADR 0042 stand; this ADR records the readings above.
