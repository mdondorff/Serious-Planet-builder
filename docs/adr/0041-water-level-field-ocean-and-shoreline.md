# 0041 Water: level field, ocean connectivity, Gerstner waves, shoreline
Status: proposed (sketch by opus-planner, 2026-10-15, for M5, from the owner's M5 design constraints of 2026-10-15; revised after the spec-guardian and numerics reviews; decided under the owner's delegation of 2026-10-12 once SPIKE-08 is done; the bake reading is ADR 0027, corrected by opus-planner 2026-10-15)
Date: 2026-10-15
Source: `docs/status.md` M5 section (owner's constraints; Gerstner only; ocean <= 2 ms); architecture report M5 row and risk register (coastline flicker); ADR 0026 (bake; point 5 open); ADR 0035, ADR 0038, ADR 0039, ADR 0040, ADR 0042; numerics review 2026-10-15 (by hand); constitution CON-04, CON-07, CON-08, CON-11, CON-12, CON-13, CON-14

## Context
The terrain has an ocean floor (elevation negative below sea level); the bake marks land by continentalness against `SEA_LEVEL_C` (ADR 0026 point 3). M5 adds a water surface; the owner's constraints keep lakes, rivers, deltas and other liquids possible without rework.

## Decision (proposed)
1. **Water-level field W(dir)** (a field under CON-07 and CON-14): the water surface height above the reference surface where there is water, absent elsewhere, plus a water-body id and kind. M5's only producer is the sea: W = 0 where the point is ocean-connected and the terrain is below it. **Reading:** the bake is read under ADR 0027 (accepted; it decides ADR 0026 point 5): land is the interpolated continentalness against `SEA_LEVEL_C` (ADR 0027 point 5). The ocean-connectivity channel (point 2) is categorical and is not interpolated: a fine point is sea when the interpolated continentalness says sea **and** at least one of the lattice vertices with a non-zero weight in ADR 0027 point 2 (the stored vertex itself at weight 0) is ocean-connected. The set of vertices is the same from either face, so the rule is edge-symmetric; the change that builds W pins it with known answers, including a face-edge point and a dry basin one cell from the ocean.
2. **Ocean connectivity in the bake (SPIKE-08 first):** a set function, independent of order: a vertex is below sea level by the stored continentalness, strict `<=` against `SEA_LEVEL_C` (the complement of ADR 0026's land rule); components by 4-connectivity over an integer cross-face adjacency table with shared face-border vertices as single nodes (3 neighbours at the 8 cube corners); every component above an area threshold (data) is ocean; components are labelled by their minimum vertex id (future lake ids depend on it); computed by tiled union-find with min-id roots or an explicit-queue fill, never recursion. SPIKE-08 measures cost and memory at 16384 cells per face edge (a visited bitset is about 200 MB) and proves serial and tiled runs bit-identical (by hash). A new bake channel: `GLOBAL_VERSION` bump, known answers. Known limit: at bake resolution (about 0.6 km) a dry basin can sit one cell from the ocean; the test set includes such a case and its expected look.
3. **Liquid kinds:** kind ids are world data (the definition); their appearance (colour and absorption, emission, roughness, wave parameters) is look data (ADR 0042 point 6). Emission writes into the HDR target (ADR 0040 point 8).
4. **Water surface on the terrain's own grid:** the CPU selects the water tiles (terrain tiles whose height range reaches below W); the water surface is drawn with the same grid, LOD and morph as the terrain tile, its vertex = terrain-local position + up x (W - h_morph) (small tile-local quantities, CON-11; never dir x (R + W) - origin). Cracks are handled as the terrain's (skirts).
5. **Gerstner waves (presentation data):** each wave has a 3D wave vector and phase on the planet-fixed position; the CPU computes in f64 per tile and wave `phi_tile = (k . P_origin + phi_0) mod 2 pi` and per frame `(omega t) mod 2 pi` with t from the `FramePlan` (CON-04); the shader adds only `k . p_local`, and amplitude fades to zero (on continuous distance or the continuous morph factor, never the discrete LOD) before `|k . p_local|` exceeds about 1e3 rad; the wave vector is projected onto the local tangent, so tiles and cube faces do not seam. Declared bound: displacement within 1 mm + 1e-3 x sum of amplitudes against an f64 evaluation at the same f32-rounded inputs and CPU-reduced phases (tier B; WGSL guarantees sin and cos only to about 5e-4 absolute); a seam test (one point through two adjacent tiles, including across a face edge) and a long-run test at t = 1e6 s. If boats ever float, waves become a field first (ADR 0035 point 4).
6. **Shoreline without flicker:** water class and the blend band (wet surface, foam, shallow transparency) come per pixel from the interpolated varying `W - h_morph` (vertical depth, from tile data), never from a depth test between two meshes or from along-ray thickness; band edges filtered with `fwidth`; depth-buffer thickness only for absorption. Depth is `Depth32Float` reversed-Z, asserted (CON-12). A scripted coastal flight measures flicker as the share of pixels that change water class between consecutive frames after reprojection with the f64 camera poses; budget 0.1 % of covered pixels per frame (provisional, confirmed by the first run).
7. **Retrofit test:** a synthetic lake (a basin with a fixed level, its own id and kind) from a test-only producer renders through the same path with no change to the water pass.
8. **Budget** (owner, 2026-10-15): water pass <= 2 ms GPU at the standard views, in a valid perf window.
9. **Time box and fallback:** SPIKE-08 one day; water pass four days; fallback: no waves (flat water, shoreline unchanged), reported.

## Consequences
- Hydrology later adds producers of W and carving; the water pass and shoreline do not change.
- A new bake channel and a field (W); goldens for ocean, shoreline, the synthetic lake.
- FFT waves come after the M5 gate behind the same wave interface.
