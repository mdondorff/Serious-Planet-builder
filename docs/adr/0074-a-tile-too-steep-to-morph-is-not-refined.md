# 0074 A tile too steep to morph is not refined; the plan degrades locally
Status: proposed (sonnet-explorer, 2026-10-21, after two rounds of spec-guardian and numerics review; the planner decides). Amends LOD-008 (the rejection of a plan with an empty morph interval); extends ADR 0014 and ADR 0016.
Date: 2026-10-21
Source: SPIKE-26 (`spikes/owner-items-sweep/results/results.md`); LOD-004, LOD-007, LOD-008, LOD-011, STRM-015, STRM-016; VIEW-032, VIEW-037; owner test 2026-10-20 (stability first)

## Context
- A leaf needs a morph interval with room (LOD-008, `first_empty_morph_interval`); its start grows with the height extent of its same-level neighbours. Terrain steeper than the level allows leaves none and the plan is rejected whole.
- The owner's stacks of 44 raise strokes make 220 m spikes (about 73 degrees); the bounds are exact, so no bound is loose. Since VIEW-037 a rejected plan keeps the last plan: one spike in view freezes the refinement of the whole view (22 of 77 items, SPIKE-26).
- The 2:1 balance and the morph shader assume that a leaf with a finer neighbour is at least its split distance from the camera; a leaf held back from splitting is nearer, so its finer neighbours would leave a T-junction at the border.
- The frame-path cover (STRM-016) repeats the plan's split test without a field and must equal the plan node for node; the draw set must stay inside the residency set R (LOD-007, STRM-015).

## Decision
1. **One predicate.** `morph_room` is the test of `LodParams::validate`, of the selection and of the backstop; it reads the raw volumes, and a non-finite bound is no room (before any `max`). A tile above the render floor is morphable when its interval from its four same-level neighbours' volumes has room. It depends on the tile alone, never on the camera or the frustum.
2. **Capping.** `C` is the set of unmorphable tiles met by the position-only walk (no frustum; padded by the ceiling's reach where the plan walks with one). The ceiling at a tile `x` is `min over u in C of (level(u) - 1 + d(x, u))`; `d` is the number of tile edges between the level-`level(u)` tiles by `TileId::neighbor` (across cube faces; for a coarser `x` the least over its descendants, for a finer `x` that of its ancestor), so the ceiling is 1-Lipschitz and balancing cannot exceed it.
3. **Plan.** The plan is the walk clamped to the ceiling by merging, then the split-only balance of today. A merged leaf or balance split that is unmorphable joins `C` and everything repeats from the walk (`C` only grows), so the result is unique, independent of order and thread count, and the draw set stays inside R.
4. **Seams.** A leaf whose neighbour across an edge is a coarser leaf sets that edge in a `stitch` mask of its node, and a leaf whose neighbour is finer sets it in a `fine` mask; the vertex shader sets the morph factor to 1 on the border vertices (skirts included) of stitched edges and to 0 on those of fine edges. Both are camera-free, so a reused plan stays crack-free. The interval is never narrowed; cover and stand-in nodes carry no mask (T-junctions beside a held leaf remain there, as the skirts hide them today). Rejected: capping every tile within the split distance of a held leaf (a spike 1 km away would cap the camera's own ground).
5. **Cover and counter.** The plan and the tile bounds carry `capped` per node and tile, meaning the split is forbidden by the final ceiling; `cover_selection_masked` stops there, so with every tile of R resident the cover equals the plan node for node. `capped_tiles` is the number of plan leaves that are ancestors of leaves of the position-only selection with its balance, counted on the final set; it is a plan field and the F3 `tiles` and `mesh` overlays draw those tiles in their own colour (a spike outside the view can cap a tile in it). A log line states the count once per 5 s, one cause, no crash snapshot (VIEW-040).
6. **No memo.** Morphable, `C`, the ceiling and `capped` are recomputed from the cached volumes each plan, never stored per tile, so an edit of a neighbour cannot leave them stale (LOD-009).
7. **Relaxed, stated.** At a capped tile the leaf under the camera misses `tau_px`, the drawn surface can lie above the true ground (a chord over a spike: the near-plane clearance of LOD-008 is not met there), and the coarsest mesh depends on the terrain (LOD-005 reads its sagitta).
8. **Refusal** stays for parameters without room at any slope and as the backstop after the selection.

## Consequences
- A steep spike is drawn at the finest level that can morph, its flank smoothed; the rest of the view refines and no plan is frozen.
- Changes: `lod.rs` (selection, cover, residency), the plan and its text (new fields written only when nonzero, so a plan with none keeps its old text and hash; the format version rises), the draw record, the compact and reference vertex shaders (the masks, in a spare lane of the node record where one exists, else the 208 B node record, its 228 B per-node pin and the terrain-share model move), the overlay, LOD-006, LOD-008 (the rejection, its scenario, the camera guarantee), LOD-009, STRM-015, STRM-016, VIEW-014, VIEW-032 (poses are capped, not refused) and the tests that expect a refusal on steep ground. Tests that assert "no refusal" to guard the stamp bounds (GEN-026, `stamp_steep`) assert `capped_tiles == 0`, so a loose bound still shows.
- Cost: up to 12 bounds per split candidate; the flat-field shortcut of `morph_interval_for` applies only at coarse levels on a real field; the plan job also walks R's candidates and the position-only selection. A perf window times the plan job: no more than 10 % over today's on SPIKE-26's items.
- The selection flags where a cap stopped a split, so `capped_tiles` needs no second walk. Tests (a spike field, the border-vertex oracle on held leaves, cover equals plan with the balance ring, draw set inside R for a spike beyond the frustum, permuted orders, NaN bounds, the edge of `validate`, a warm plan equal to a cold one after a neighbour edit) are in the change's design notes.
- Easy to reverse: the passes act only where a tile is unmorphable; both masks are 0 elsewhere.
- Cost of the decision: about four to five coder days in three changes (selection, cover and plan; the shader masks; overlay and specs).
