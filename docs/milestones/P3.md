## E3 pass bars (lead, 2026-11-15, on the owner's "set bars"; fixed before any B-F result; variant A's R1h numbers were known)
Rules:
- G-DRAIN: river cells in closed basins (BUILD-055: at least 0.5 m deep and 16 cells) at most 10 %, on every window. A modelling bar, not an Earth match: Earth DTMs keep real pits (3DEP 7-45 %).
- G-INCISION: the valley-depth share (BUILD-055: surface 1-2 cells out more than 50 m above the river cell) at most the largest share among the benchmark tiles whose 10 x 10 relief is 1 to 2 times the window's start-surface relief (BUILD-055's Detail; benchmark of SPIKE-119, #1525). The incised-plateau tile (N36 W113) is left out: canyons only on purpose (owner, 2026-11-13). 3DEP replaces GLO-30 where both exist.
- Cross-sections: on each window's transects T1-T3, no valley narrower and deeper than its matched Earth valley at one vertical scale; the lead pre-reads, the owner judges the figures.
- A variant passes when all three hold on all five windows. A bar changes only with the owner's word and a written reason.

## D-1 choice among passing variants (owner, 2026-11-15; set after the D-F and B-C results)
The bars of the previous section do not separate the variants: A, B, D, E and F pass both on all five windows; C fails G-DRAIN on M1.
Required, in addition to the bars: the trunk corridor.
- Every H1 trunk reach can be routed inside a corridor of k_c = 2 coarse cells (about 1.6 km) without a rise above its running minimum over the window's rise bar: min(50 m, max(10 m, 0.5 x the 10 x 10 start relief)), which is R1h 27.5 m, R3a 10 m, R3b 10 m, M1 50 m, F1 10 m.
- Confluences snap to the lowest L cell within 1 coarse cell, shared by their reaches.
- A border crossing passes through the lowest L cell on the edge within 1 H1 cell of H1's crossing.
- The pass share is fixed by the owner from explorer's #158 numbers (raw and snapped), before the D-1 choice.
- If every variant fails on M1 even snapped, that becomes a D-2 requirement (H1 or KR adjusts on mountains), not a D-1 blocker.

Then the tie-break, in order:
1. no ridge cut-through (owner, 2026-11-13);
2. least ground lowered over 50 m below the start surface;
3. relief at 1 km closest to the start;
4. bake cost.

Reported, not required: edge agreement within 2 km (unconstrained L routing; low on flat windows for every variant and for the start surface).
