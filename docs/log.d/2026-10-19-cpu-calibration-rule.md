- 2026-10-19 **cpu-calibration-rule archived (TEST-039 added; ADR 0065):** a perf scene's verdicts count only if its CPU calibration (STRM-022) lies within limits taken from the quiet-machine spread, before and after the case: single 52,000 us before and 56,000 after, parallel median 112,000 (18 threads); outside them the scene gets no verdict (`no verdict: CPU calibration of '<scene>' ...` in the log, `frame_valid`, the console and `accept`); the log carries `cpu_calibration_valid` and its reasons; the lit views and flights now record the calibration too; logs without one are not judged; the prose rule in status.md is a pointer; the old limits (50,000 / 100,000) would have voided about one clean scene in five (opus-planner's data request; sonnet-crusader; tier-A tests on boundaries, old logs and an `accept` refusal).
