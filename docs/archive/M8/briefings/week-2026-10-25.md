## Coordination
- **File ownership:** explorer owns generators (field chain, chunk stage, A-net), `frame/lod.rs`, `streaming`, `app/worldfield.rs`; crusader owns the global stage, stored channels, `render`, `perf/` and the perf scenes. Crossing needs the planner's OK and a small PR of its own; the owner of the file merges main before touching it again.
- **IDs:** a merged proposal claims its requirement IDs, cache `Kind`s and params; the next change starts above them (as of 2026-10-24: crusader holds GEN-044..046, STRM-045, STRM-048, REND-038, REND-041, Kind 9 and 10, PARAM 12; explorer holds GEN-047..055, STRM-046/047, REND-039/040, LOD-014). Check main before claiming.
- **Perf windows:** the planner opens one when every other lane is idle or on docs-only work. Protocol: the coder writing docs sends "proposal started"; the planner sends "window open" to the runner; the runner reports and says "run ended"; the planner sends "window closed".
- **Pace:** two lanes about 1 % of the weekly quota per hour, about 0.25 % per PR (2026-10-24).
